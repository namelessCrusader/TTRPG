1. THE SHORT ANSWER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ledger is honest and mostly well formed, but it records THE SYMPTOMS of a few early choices rather than the choices. Read as a whole it
says six things:

A. THE BIGGEST REALISM DEBT IS STILL A GATE THAT IS OFF BY DEFAULT: gas.momentum (gasflow.py:110 GAS_MOMENTUM = False). While it is off, hot air carries
   heat by the EDDY stand-in (materials.py:553 EDDY 7500; heat.py:1599), and realism-map section 1 shows the consequence: the room fire is upside down
   (121 C at the floor, 28 C under the ceiling at 30 s). Four more ledger rows (gas.eddy_heat, gas.drag_mom, gas.mix_mom, cold.plume_k) hang off it. It is
   "scheduled" only in the sense of W15-Q8 (all laws on, by exact optimisation and Rust). No wave names the flip. (v) NEW as a named task.
B. SEVERAL "CRUTCHES" ARE CONSERVATION HOLES, not shortcuts. The owner's rule is "every joule booked". Rows that break it today: cold.matter_rule_partial,
   wet.seep_no_heat, push.work_unbooked, contact.adj_ignored, layers.temp_clip, body.work_billing, mix.steam_to_gone, layers.soot_no_thermal, heat_history.scale_unbooked.
   Some are tiny (3e-5 of the host), but cold.matter_rule_partial and push.work_unbooked are structural. A crutch that creates or destroys energy should be
   ranked above a crutch that merely looks wrong.
C. THE ONE-NUMBER-PER-MATERIAL LEGACY IS STILL ALIVE in three places: friction 0.40 (contact.pivot_dry_040, mat.friction_voxel_only, materials.py:485),
   MPM strengths inverted (stone 8 MPa below wood 10 MPa, mat.mpm_inversion), and SPAN as a veto floor (mat.span_floor). All three are "D5 unify" and none is in a
   named wave. They are cheap (data) and visible (boulders weaker than logs, soil that slides with the wrong angle of repose).
D. THE PERSON IS STILL A RIGID, VELOCITY-LESS THING driven by pushes: motion._v_push, contact.slip_speed, stairs.lunge, contact.step_friction (binary),
   injury.landing_mode, injury.fractures_unread. This is the same class of early choice as the owner's rigid-voxel decision (a rigid thing standing in for a
   compliant one). Waves 15 (stairs, grip, fall law) and 16a (primitives) touch parts of it, but nothing schedules a person with real velocity, legs and
   compliance. That is the next "rigid shape-keeping" in the tree. (u) The structural fix is a lane for another auditor; I list the ledger evidence.
E. FLOAT32 LATTICE ENERGY (sim.py:191-194 E_dtype float32, "and every golden hash"). A flesh voxel holds 7400 J and a float32 step there is 5e-4 J; the ledger
   already has faintrates.fresh_band (float32 o2 field) as a symptom and layers.temp_clip (a NaN from "a stray negative E") as another possible one.
   BodyWorld sets float64 locally. The choice made the goldens cheap; it costs real accuracy for slow warm things and hides drift behind the books' tolerance
   (drift 0.002 g in the fuzz). NEW (not in the ledger as a row at all). OWNER-LEVEL (cost: memory bandwidth, golden rebase).
F. The crutches whose replacement law is GENUINELY SCHEDULED are healthy: bleeding, burn faces, grip, doors, quench film, thin-plate area, shut-room star, tissue
   repair, heat history, reaction runner. About 25 rows resolve when waves 15 batches 2-4 and 16a land. After merging batch 2 the ledger needs a sweep (section 4).

TOP FIVE (details in section 2):
  1. Flip and finish gas momentum (retire EDDY, drag_mom, plume_k, eddy_heat) and book the gas kinetic energy (push.work_unbooked).
  2. Close the energy holes in the ledger: matter_rule_partial, seep_no_heat, temp_clip, adj_ignored, work_billing (a conservation sweep, not five features).
  3. Unify the material record in the MPM and the pivot: friction pairs and the wood/stone strength inversion, then delete SPAN (D5).
  4. Clothing and cold: clothing_lattice_path, pour_at_20C, b0_fixed (outdoor humidity), film_no_ice. Winter play is wrong without them and each is small.
  5. Retire the rigid person: fractures_unread (a broken leg still bears load), slip_speed/_v_push, step_friction binary, lunge.

2.1 GAS MOMENTUM STILL OFF BY DEFAULT (and the four rows that depend on it)
  Choice: gasflow.py:110 GAS_MOMENTUM = False; heat.py:1599 uses the EDDY eddy conductivity when off (zero when on); materials.py:553 EDDY 7500;
          GAS_DRAG_MOM 0.1/s (gasflow.py:113), GAS_ENTRAIN 0.03 (gasflow.py:89, the same number used as momentum mixing, (u)); climate.plume_k 100 K (climate.py:90).
  Why: the 20 cm gas field was lossy and pumped mass when it tried to carry O2 (realism-map 3.1); the momentum lane was built in 14a behind a gate and
       left off while the speed and the scene regressions are measured.
  Realism cost: buoyant plumes, ceiling jets, hot layers, doorway neutral plane, "crawl low", flashover and banking down cannot come out right; the fire
       is hottest at the floor (v, realism-map). Wind and bellows are the push law's job but that law rides the same flow. Smoke, CO and O2 do not stratify.
  Better: turn it on and delete the stand-ins: a low-Mach buoyant projection with limited advection (what the lane built), wall shear and a turbulence
       closure (Smagorinsky; Pope, Turbulent Flows, 2000 (k)) instead of the linear GAS_DRAG_MOM; validate against Heskestad plume, Alpert ceiling jet,
       McCaffrey and Zukoski filling (k) (the realism-map tests, already listed).
  Cost/risk: the lane measured 0.7-1.25 ms with unconverged iterations; expect 2-5 ms (realism-map v). Risk: all goldens change (declared diffs). The Rust
       port of the pressure solve is the Q8 route.
  Scheduled?  PARTLY: Decision 4 14a lane D built it; "gate flips" are W15 batch 3c ("default-on flips") but no spec names gas.momentum, and gas.momentum's own
       deleted_when says "gas_flow is on by default in every scene and all of them have passed with momentum on" (a condition nobody owns). Mark: NEW as an owned task.
  Priority: HIGH (the largest single realism term for fire scenes, which are 12% of book options).
  Owner-level?: technical, except the cost ceiling (a few ms) which is Q8 already decided.
  deleted_when check: right, but the four dependents should be listed in ONE row's condition so they go together.

2.2 ENERGY HOLES (crutches that break "every joule booked")
  Choices (file:line from the ledger text; rows in section 3):
    - cold.matter_rule_partial: burn, char, boil, melt, freeze, spawn and scenes.fill change capacity at fixed E; only things._unmake books it.
    - wet.seep_no_heat: seep moves vapour grams without their heat.
    - push.work_unbooked: push work on the gas is in a stats counter, the gas kinetic energy is not in the books.
    - contact.adj_ignored: a hot layer moved by a foot or hand does not book its heat.
    - layers.temp_clip: temperature clipped to [-150, 5000] C because "a stray negative E made a NaN": this silently creates energy.
    - body.work_billing: lift, swing, throw and climb heat comes from nowhere (14b G probably retired most of this; see 4.).
    - mix.steam_to_gone: steam of a mixed boil is booked to the gone book, not to a vapour store (water mass leaves the water cycle).
    - heat_history.scale_unbooked: oxide scale grows without booking oxygen or iron.
  Why: each was a lane's pragmatic cut so a law could land with the books closing at the golden tolerance.
  Realism cost: a player never sees a joule, but long runs drift (fuzz drift 0.002 g is fine today; a month of play and a campfire in a closed room are not
       checked), a cold-climate world with melting and freezing gains or loses heat at every phase change that is not through _unmake, and quenching or forging
       gets energy from nowhere. More important, the owner's faithfulness test for magic ("hand-off equivalence: a deposit of Q J must give the same downstream
       physics as an ordinary Q J source", Decision magic FAITHFULNESS) needs the books to be true: it cannot be tested on a leaky ledger.
  Better: one invariant instead of eight fixes: every law that changes capacity, mass or species calls ONE "matter_change" gate that moves the sensible heat
       with the matter (the C1 spec already names outside.matter_change) and the audit fails any law that writes E, mass or species outside it. See Noether-style
       "conservation by construction" in the lattice-Boltzmann and FV literature; the practical pattern is the flux-form update (k): every transfer is a signed flux
       between two cells, so the sum is exact. A static AST lint (writes to E/mass arrays only inside whitelisted functions) is cheap.
  Cost/risk: runtime ~0. Work: a medium refactor (about 10 laws). Risk: golden diffs.
  Scheduled?: matter_rule_partial says "C3 and C2 migrate them one by one": NO wave names it (W15 batches have reactions, smithing, doors, bleeding, grip, twins,
       quench, healing). NEW as a sweep.
  Priority: HIGH (tiny player-visible effect now, but it blocks magic faithfulness, long runs, and the owner's rule).
  Owner-level?: technical (the owner's standing rule already decides it).
  deleted_when check: right for each, but the real condition is the single invariant above; one row should own "the energy audit has zero exceptions".

2.3 THE ONE-RECORD LEGACY: friction 0.40, MPM strengths, SPAN

          mat.mpm_inversion (matrec legacy MPM rows: wood 10 MPa above stone 8 MPa); mat.span_floor (SPAN veto floor, decision D5).
  Why: SPAN and 0.40 were the first support and friction laws; the matfric table (matfric.py:68-75, many (u) pairs) arrived in 13b and was wired only to the
       voxel _effort and to floor_mu. The MPM is a separate solver with its own strength column.
  Realism cost: sand, gravel and snow piles stand at the wrong angle of repose (the MPM wall friction is a constant); a boulder thrown at a log shatters first;
       a stone arch or lintel is judged by SPAN first and only vetoed by the beam check; the crate-push and ledge scenes agree with each other by calibration, not by physics.
       A loaded structure (a roof with people on it, snow load) can stand because SPAN says so (SPAN is load-blind; the veto is for changes only: mat.veto_changed_cells).
  Better: one material record read by every solver (the realism-map P1 "one material record"; the matrec ingest already exists); in the MPM a per-node wall friction
       field (Coulomb, Hu et al. MLS-MPM 2018 (k), and Klar et al. 2016 for sand with Drucker-Prager (k)); beam check for horizontal runs and arch thrust-line check
       (Heyman 1966, thrust-line analysis (k)) to replace SPAN; unify the numbers by sourced table (Wood Handbook, Engineering Toolbox, CES; (k)).
  Cost/risk: data lookups, a 3-6 ms layout-change check (realism-map v). Risk: golden diffs; calcite has no MPM row yet (1d bug).
  Scheduled?: NEW as a named wave task (D5 is "one by one"; W5b is old; 2f only adds calcite). The owner's new rod/elastic decision needs this record anyway:
       E and strength per material must be right before stiffness-from-moduli means anything. So this is the PREREQUISITE of the deformation decision.
  Priority: HIGH (cheap, visible, and a dependency).
  Owner-level?: technical. The SPAN retirement alone is owner-level (user wanted to review overhangs: W9 per the row).
  deleted_when check: span_floor's condition (user retires SPAN after arch detection and a review) is right and owner-gated; friction_voxel_only's W5b is stale
       naming (no wave 5b is in the plan); rewrite to "the MPM reads a per-node wall friction from the record".

2.6 FLOAT32 ENERGY AND THE (u)-FLOAT FIELD TRIPS
  Choice: sim.py:191-194 E_dtype = np.float32 for every world except warm-body worlds; faintrates.FRESH_BAND 0.95 (o2 float32); layers.temp_clip; the float64-for-bodies rule.
  Why: memory and golden-hash stability.
  Realism cost: a resting body's energy step is 5e-4 J (comment at sim.py:191-193); slow processes (a body cooling in a night) lose small drains to rounding; fresh air
       "reads as before within 5 percent" so a stale room's 18 percent O2 is read as 20.9 by the curve. This is a measurable but hidden error source.
  Better: float64 E everywhere (2x memory on E; the heat sweep is bandwidth-bound so expect ~1.3-1.6x on that sweep (u)), or float32 with a compensated (Kahan) update

  Cost/risk: the owner's tick is 25 ms; the E sweep is a share of it (measure). Risk: every golden hash.
  Scheduled?: NEW, not in the ledger.
  Priority: MEDIUM.
  Owner-level?: YES (a trade between memory, speed and accuracy, and it forces a base rebase).

Five ledger rows move or must move with it:

  - stairs.lunge, motion._v_push: a stiff person is a rigid person; a bending trunk and a rigid man on it will give wrong sway frequencies if the load is a point pusher.
The deformation decision therefore depends on #3 of the top five. I recommend scheduling it directly after the material unification.
