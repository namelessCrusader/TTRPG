early engine choices that could be done differently or better for realism. This is lane B.
Each entry gives: the choice (file:line), why it was made, what realism it costs, the better
alternatives with sources, cost and real-time risk, whether it is scheduled or NEW, a
priority (impact over cost), and whether it is an OWNER-LEVEL choice or technical.

Claim marks: (v) verified by reading the code or the owner's records in this session;
(k) known from the literature or from memory, not re-checked this session; (u) unverified
or an estimate. No web search was used; every (k) source is a standard reference named so
it can be fetched through the library (Babbel) before building.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE SHORT VERSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The heat, gas and fluid laws are mostly sound conservation laws on a 5 cm lattice with a
20 cm gas grid. The early choices that still cost the most realism fall into four families:

 (1) THINGS THAT ARE BUILT BUT OFF. The buoyant gas flow and its momentum are off by
     default (air.py:15, gasflow.py GAS_MOMENTUM False), so a default world still uses the
     old stand-ins (a capped rise, a constant ceiling jet, an eddy-conductivity term).
     W15-Q8 already says "every law on in every world"; no batch names this flip.
 (2) ONE-NUMBER STAND-INS WHERE A FIELD SHOULD BE. Light is one lux number per world.
     A room is ONE radiant node. The sun heats nothing (shortwave is zero). Sound is one
     level in one band. Smoke is one scalar for "all visible products". Convection is
     one constant h. Ambient noise is one number per world.
 (3) THE CELL AS THE UNIT OF SHAPE. Face area is sqrt(fill) (a rod), a curved surface is
     counted by staircase faces, a sub-cell thing has no thickness. The thin-plate fix is
     scheduled (W15 batch 3); the curved-surface part and the missing shape class are NEW.
 (4) COMBUSTION AS A SURFACE FLUX, NOT A GAS-PHASE PROCESS. No fuel vapour, no flash point
     or flammability limit, no flammable gas, no explosion, no smoulder, no sealed-room
     pressure. Many DCC-type scenes (188 bomb and explosive cases in the map) need them.

TOP FIVE (details in sections 1 to 4):
 1. B7  Turn on buoyant gas flow plus momentum in every world and delete the stand-ins.
 2. B2  Radiant exchange between all surfaces (patches and view factors), with the sun as
        a shortwave source; remove the "room is one node" and the 500 C source floor.
 3. B21 A real light field (photometric, spectral bands) sharing the patch system of B2,
        read by sight (contrast, not a flat optical-depth cut).
 4. B9/B13/B8 Fuel vapour and gas species (soot, CO, CO2, steam) with flash point,
        flammability limits and sealed-room pressure: one gas-phase combustion path.
 5. B1  Sub-voxel shape for heat: surface-to-volume per cell (plates, rods, powder) and a
        staircase correction for curved surfaces; the thin-plate fix is already booked.

1. HEAT AND RADIATION

B1. FACE AREA PER CELL (SUB-VOXEL SHAPE FOR HEAT)
Choice: a face's heat exchange uses the cell's face area A = dx^2 times af = sqrt(fill) for a
partly filled solid (heat.py:1161, comment at heat.py:1033: "its surface is sqrt(fill) of the
faces, about a square rod's"). A curved or oblique surface is the sum of its axis-aligned
exposed faces. (v)
Why: one rule, no geometry stored; cheap; fine for sticks and coal.
Realism cost: (a) a thin PLATE (a blade, a sheet of iron, a shield) is not a rod: its broad
face is the whole cell face while its fill is small, so sqrt(fill) is wrong in a way the
owner already flagged ("the lattice overstates a thin plate's air area", decisions.md 2026-11-22;
every quench scene changes); (b) powder, straw, foliage and hair have surface-to-volume
ratios of 10 to 1000 times a rod's, so they heat, dry and ignite too slowly; (c) a sloped
or round surface counted by staircase faces over-counts area by 1.27 for a cylinder and up to
1.5 to 1.73 for a plane at 45 deg (Manhattan metric: the exposed-face sum is A_true times
(|nx|+|ny|+|nz|)); so a round pot, a sphere of iron, a person's limb and a slope shed and
take heat too fast; (d) the sky-view and radiative coupling inherit the same stair-step.
Alternatives: (1) store ONE extra number with a partly filled cell's matter: its shape class
and characteristic thickness (slab t, rod d, powder grain d), from which S/V and the broad
and edge areas follow exactly; the sub-voxel 1-D conduction already exists in thinheat.py,
so the area and the thickness are the only missing data (v). (2) For full cells, scale an
exposed face by the surface normal from the fill gradient: weights from the voxel surface-area
estimation literature (Mullikin and Verbeek 1993; Lindblad 2005; (k)), so a cylinder's area
comes out within a few percent; 1/(|nx|+|ny|+|nz|) is the simplest exact rule for a plane.
(3) A cut-cell area (a fraction of the face open) read from the fill field, the way gas
cut-cell solvers do (Bridson, Fluid Simulation for Computer Graphics; (k)).
Cost and risk: a float32 per surface cell and a table; the heat face kernel is already
numba-compiled (heat.py:205) so the change is a multiplier; golden diffs on every scene with
a round or thin thing (declared, as the owner already accepts for the plate). Real-time risk
low (about 0 ms).
Status: the thin-plate part is SCHEDULED (W15 batch 3, owner YES). The staircase part (c) and
the general shape class (b) are NEW; fold them into the same batch so every quench and forge
scene changes once, not twice.
Priority: high. Owner-level: no (the plate fix is owner-decided; the rest is technical).

B2. RADIATION: SOURCES, VIEW FACTORS, THE SUN, THE ONE-NODE ROOM
Choice: radiation across air is thrown only from fires (as points) and from surfaces hotter
than 500 C (RAD_HOT = 500, heat.py:~107), the strongest 24 sources (RAD_MAX_SRC), every 6 ticks
(RAD_EVERY), by an inverse-square law with a cos factor and optical depth along the ray; a
receiver absorbs emissivity times what lands, and nothing is reflected. (v, heat.py:_law_radiate,
_throw). Outdoors, a surface loses heat to an environment temperature that mixes sky and air by
sky view; the absorbed shortwave `sw` is passed as 0.0 (outside_world.py:328 and 343; outside.py
docstring "zero in T8"). Indoors, the room interior is ONE radiant node (outside.py header:
"Known limit (spec 3.7): the interior of a room is ONE radiant node (mrt_star), with no view
factors between its surfaces"). (v)
Why: the first radiation (G7) had to give a fire its reach for a few ms; the cold world (T8)
needed room heat balance without a view-factor matrix; the sun was left as a seam.
Realism cost: (a) a 300 C stove, a hot kettle, an iron stove-pipe or a sun-warmed wall gives no
radiant warmth to a person at 1 m, only through the air; real stoves warm by radiation first.
(b) THE SUN DOES NOT HEAT ANYTHING: no solar gain through a window, no sun-warmed stone, no
shade, no south and north walls, no greenhouse, no dark cloth versus white; sky radiation exists
but not sunshine. (c) No reflection: a polished reflector, foil, a shiny shield, a mirror, a
white wall bouncing firelight: none. (d) A room warmed by a wall at 40 C and a person's own
radiant loss to cold walls (the main comfort and hypothermia path indoors) is a single mean
radiant node, so standing by a cold window or in a cold stone church is wrong in direction and
size. (e) Radiation is one grey band: no selective absorption (glass passes shortwave but blocks
longwave; snow reflects sun but emits as a black body; white paint is white in light but black
in the infrared). (f) A thing between you and a fire casts a "shadow" only through the line
optical depth, so a person's body, a shield or a screen works, but a big wall of fire at a
short distance is a point source (error at near field: a 3 m flame at 1 m is not a point).
Alternatives: (1) ZONAL RADIOSITY ON 20 CM PATCHES (Hottel and Sarofim, Radiative Transfer;
Modest, Radiative Heat Transfer; Siegel and Howell; (k)): treat exposed faces as patches grouped
to 20 cm (same grid as the gas), compute a sparse view-factor set per room on a geometry change
(Monte Carlo rays or hemicube, as in Cohen and Wallace's radiosity text; (k)), solve a few
Gauss-Seidel sweeps at 1 Hz or on change; booked exactly because each patch pair conserves
what it sends. (2) TWO BANDS, shortwave (solar and light, 0.3 to 3 um) and longwave (thermal
IR): one absorptance column and one emissivity column per material; this is the standard
two-band building-physics split (EnergyPlus, ASHRAE Fundamentals; (k)). The solar source is
one directional light with the existing ray walker for shade; sky diffuse from sky view. (3)
Keep the current point-source throw ONLY for flames (their near field needs the finite-flame
view factor: the solid-flame model of Mudan and of Heskestad (k)), and let all other emitters
(stoves, hot metal, walls) be patches. (4) Reflection by a few radiosity bounces (diffuse) and
one specular term for polished metal, glass, water.
Cost and real-time risk: patches per room 300 to 2000; the set of view factors is n^2 floats
(about 4 MB for 1000 patches) and stored sparsely beyond 10 m; rebuild only on geometry change;
the solve is a few ms at 1 Hz, so about 0.05 ms per tick amortised (u, to be measured). The
solar term is one ray walk per surface patch per sun-step (every 10 sim minutes outdoors).
Risk: the "room is one node" simplification was chosen for the cold world's cost; patches
raise the per-room state, so use the SAME lazy-region machinery as the gas store. The two bands
double the columns but not the loops.
Status: forge cavity radiation in packed beds was 14a C (done, narrow). All of the above is
NEW; the map lists "Radiant heat across air" (done as G7) but not the sun, reflection or room
exchange. The shortwave band is also the SAME patch set as light (B21), so build them together.
Priority: high. Owner-level: technical, except one real choice: two bands (cheap, standard) or
the spectral bands that magic's waste light needs (decision Q1 asks for colour, so at least 3 to
4 bands for shortwave); recommend 2 thermal plus 4 shortwave bands once, not twice.

B3. HEAT STATE: FLOAT32 ENERGY ABOVE 20 C, EXPLICIT CLIPPED FACE FLUX
Choice: E is float32 joules above 20 C (outside.py header; sim.py:256 E_dtype); every face flux
is explicit and clipped to FACE_CLIP = 0.15 of the smaller side's heat capacity times the
difference (materials.py:557); pending float64 arrays (reactions.py) and booked "quant" ledgers
repair what float32 loses. (v)
Why: speed and memory at 0.4 to 5 M voxels; the clip guarantees no cell passes its neighbour.
Realism cost: (a) float32 loses small drains: the realism map notes "float32 loses a resting
drain entirely" and the reaction runner needed a pending store to commit slow rows (v);
(b) the clip changes timescales where a face is stiff: a high-h face (quench 4000 W/m2K, forge
radiation) on a thin cell hits the clip and the flux falls below the law's value, so the first
seconds of a quench or a forge heat are slower than the law says and depend on the tick (the
map recorded a voxel quench about 100x too slow before 2f; the film fix hid, not removed, the
clip); (c) heat of a tick cannot cross more than the clip, so a hot gas parcel entering a cold
cell at 1000 K difference is under-transferred and the remainder is carried by later ticks.
Alternatives: (1) float64 E in the active window only (the lazy-array machinery from 14a and
W15 1a already restricts work to boxes), keeping float32 cold storage; or Kahan-compensated
sums in the Rust kernel; (2) a semi-implicit face update where the clip bites: for a pair of
cells the exact exponential relaxation of their difference over dt (the two-body analytic
solution) replaces the clip, conserves energy exactly and is unconditionally stable (Patankar,
Numerical Heat Transfer and Fluid Flow; (k)); thinheat.py already does backward Euler per
record (v), so the lattice is the odd one out.
Cost and risk: the two-body exponential is one expm1 per stiff face; only faces that hit the
clip need it; Rust bit-for-bit rule applies to the clipped path, so keep the old path as
the reference and add the new one as a declared diff. Real-time risk low to medium.
Status: NEW (W15-Q8 Rust is a speed plan, not this). Priority: medium (high once forge and
quench matter). Owner-level: no.

B4. SUB-VOXEL THIN THINGS EXIST ONLY AS "RECORDS"
Choice: heat through the thickness of a thing smaller than a cell is solved only for a named
Record (thinheat.py, records_world.py), which is a readout that reconciles with the host cell
(switches: rec.readout_only, rec.spmap iron, wood, glass only, rec.lattice_cp). (v)
Why: the lattice cell is 5 cm; blades, hides and sheets needed true time scales.
Realism cost: a record exists only where a scene or act declares one; any thin thing without
a record (a leaf, a paper, a cloth, a plank of 2 cm, a wire) uses the cell's time scale, wrong
by (50 mm / thickness)^2 (v, thinheat.py header); records hold only iron, wood, glass.
Alternative: make "sub-voxel thickness" a property of matter in a cell (see B1's shape class)
and run the same through-thickness solve for any cell whose shape class is thin, with the
slab or rod chosen by the class; records then become the same thing as a cell with a class.
One law, not a special object.
Cost and risk: the thin solve costs 0.35 to 1.7 ms per record (v); a batch of a thousand is
3 to 13 us a row, so cost is bounded by the number of THIN, HOT-OR-WET cells; put it in the
lazy window. Status: partly scheduled (W15 1b shared surfaceh; records widening is not named).
Priority: medium. Owner-level: no.

B5. CONVECTION IS A CONSTANT, NOT A FUNCTION OF GEOMETRY AND FLOW
Choice: H_CONV = 10 W/m2K to still air on every exposed face (materials.py:544); flesh alone has
a natural-convection column (FILM_COLS hnat 2.38); the face-environment overlay lets one material
carry its own hnat (heat.py:85 comment, faceenv.py). Forced convection from the gas flow is not
read; a hot gas touching a wall uses the same h. (v)
Why: h is cheap and the face law needed one number; the flow did not exist then.
Realism cost: natural convection is h = C (dT/L)^(1/4) for a vertical plate and larger facing
up than down (Churchill and Chu 1975; Incropera; (k)): a hot plate facing up loses heat 1.4x
faster than the same plate facing down, a big surface loses less per area than a small one; a
hot pot in a draught, a blade in a breeze, a forge in a bellows blast, a quenched bar in
stirred water, a person in wind (wind chill, which matters for the cold and the burn scenes)
all use h = 10 whatever the speed. A cooling body in a gale versus still air cools the same.
Alternatives: h = max(h_nat(dT, L, orientation), h_forced(|u|, L)) blended as (h_nat^3 + h_forc^3)^(1/3)
(Churchill 1977; (k)), with the local gas speed from the flow field (the face speeds exist once
B7 is on), the characteristic length from the cell shape class (B1) and the plate orientation
from the face normal (up, down, side, 3 cases). The same form works for liquids (a water bath
stirred).
Cost and risk: one cube root per exposed hot face; it lives in the numba face kernel; the
overlay table already exists. Real-time risk low. Golden diffs on every scene with an
exposed face away from 20 C.
Status: bellows forced convection was in 14a C (forge only, v); the general law is NEW. Priority:
high (it is the correct cooling law for the person, the pot and the sword). Owner-level: no.

B6. EDDY CONDUCTIVITY IN HOT GAS (A STAND-IN FOR CONVECTION)
Choice: gas to gas conductivity grows with (T-20)/EDDY, EDDY = 7500 (materials.py:553), a stand-in
for the convection a flow would carry; it is zero where gas_momentum is on (heat.py, slownet.py,
slowforms.py; switches gas.eddy_heat). (v)
Realism cost: with gas_flow off, a plume loses heat by diffusing like a solid: the realism map's
finding that a room fire at 30 s is 121 C near the floor and 28 C under the ceiling (the room is
upside down) comes from this plus the capped rise.
Alternative: delete it by turning the flow on (B7); its removal is the test.
Status: folded into B7. Priority: high with B7. Owner-level: no.

B7. THE BUOYANT GAS FLOW AND ITS MOMENTUM ARE OFF BY DEFAULT
Choice: air.py:15 GAS_FLOW = False; gasflow.py GAS_MOMENTUM False (switches: gas.momentum "off
by default, only inside gas_flow"); scenes_push.py and scenes_wet.py switch them on per scene
(v). A default world therefore runs the legacy lossy pneumatic field, `_law_rise` with its RISE_MAX
half cap, a ceiling jet "raced by a constant", and a two-zone stir for oxygen (gasflow.py header,
air.py header; v).
Why: the flow arrived in wave 13 (lane 7) and wave 14a; every golden scene was recorded on the
stand-ins; the measured cost of the box was not yet under the ceiling.
Realism cost: the "room fire is upside down" finding of the map applies to every world that has
not switched the flow on: no crawl low, no neutral plane at a doorway, no ceiling jet speed, no
plume entrainment, no wind (the push law and wind faces only work inside gas_flow), no forced
draft to a forge, no burning smoke front. Everything downstream (smoke sight, CO dose, escape
decisions, who survives a fire) rests on it.
Alternative: the decided one: flip it ON for every world once measured under the cost ceiling,
declare each diff (W15-Q8), delete `_law_rise`, the smoke rise and ceiling-jet constants, the
two-zone stir, the legacy pressure field (cold.legacy_pressure_mean crutch), body.p_follow, and
gas.eddy_heat in one wave, because they exist only to stand in for the flow. Sources: the model
is the standard Boussinesq plume and ceiling-jet tests (Heskestad, Alpert, Zukoski; v in the
header of gasflow.py as "chosen against Heskestad's plume, Zukoski's filling and the doorway").
Cost and risk: the quiet cost is the unknown; the code sleeps below GAS_QUIET_K = 0.1 K and
works only in the active box, so a world with no fire pays about nothing (u). The risk is the
2 to 8 substeps and a 400-iteration CG (GAS_P_ITERS) in a fire: measure with Rust or numba
for the projection (preconditioned CG is the hot loop; (k) Rust or a multigrid V-cycle gives 5
to 10x).
Status: W15-Q8 names the goal, no batch names this flip. RECOMMEND scheduling it as the first
wave 15 batch 3 item, before 16a, because 16b (knowledge) and magic (waste heat, light, qi
transport) use the gas carrier. Priority: high. Owner-level: the cost ceiling is the owner's
(W15-Q8 says "optimise until it fits"), the physics is not.

B8. THE GAS SOLVER'S OWN EARLY CHOICES (BOUSSINESQ, NO ROOM PRESSURE, CONSTANT PROPERTIES)
Choice (all v from the gasflow.py docstring and constants): (1) Boussinesq: density difference
only in the buoyancy term, constant AIR_DENS 1.2 and AIR_C 1.2 J/(L K) for all gas; (2) the
projection is incompressible: "a sealed room's pressure" is not in it (docstring: "Still not in
it: a turbulence model ... and a sealed room's pressure"; the sealed airspace keeps no pressure);
(3) turbulence is one constant alpha (GAS_ENTRAIN 0.03), a linear drag (GAS_DRAG 0.75, or
GAS_DRAG_MOM 0.1 with momentum, a crutch in switches); (4) momentum advection is semi-Lagrangian
with MacCormack (gasmom.py): stable but not momentum conserving, with a numerical viscosity of
about u dx/2; (5) work done by surfaces on the gas is stat only (push.work_unbooked), the flow's
kinetic energy is not on the books.
Realism cost: (a) no sealed-room overpressure: a fire in a closed room never builds the several
hundred Pa that bursts windows, slams doors or blows smoke out of cracks; a closed oven, a kettle
with a blocked spout, a sealed jar of fermenting must, a cannon bore and a pressure vessel have no
pressure; (b) no pressure waves, no blasts and no shock: a powder keg is a very fast fire (the
map calls detonation "skip"; the DCC set has 188 bomb and explosive cases); (c) variable gas
properties matter at a flame's 1000 K (density falls to 0.3, viscosity doubles, conductivity
rises 3 to 4x): these set plume speed and mixing; (d) a constant alpha mixes the same at every
speed and scale: a small flame under a metre ("a plume thinner than a cell shows as the same
heat in twice the gas", v) and a big fire get one entrainment constant; (e) a jet along a ceiling
mixes out a little each tick by the scheme's own viscosity (the doc: 30 percent error in
temperature and 1.6x in speed against Alpert, v); (f) the kinetic energy of the flow is off the
books, so "every joule booked" is broken for the largest-moving quantity (the work done by a
bellows or a wind is booked as input and not as flow energy).
Alternatives: (1) LOW-MACH WITH A THERMODYNAMIC PRESSURE (Rehm and Baum 1978; McGrattan et al.,
Fire Dynamics Simulator technical reference, (k)): one background pressure p0(t) per closed
airspace region, whose rate of change is set by net heat, inflow and leakage; the projection then
has a divergence source that does not average to zero. This gives a sealed room's pressure, flow
through cracks (a power-law leak per opening, from the door model already planned), and the
pressure cook of a kettle, cheaply (one scalar per airspace; the region labelling already exists
in air.py regions()). (2) A sub-grid model in place of a constant alpha: the Deardorff or
Smagorinsky eddy viscosity of an LES (FDS uses Deardorff; (k)), with cell size from the actual
cell. (3) Variable density and cp(T) from one table for air; density by the ideal gas at
constant p0. (4) Energy-conserving advection (staggered, flux-form kinetic energy, or add a
vorticity-confinement term; Fedkiw, Stam and Jensen 2001; (k)), or accept the loss and book the
kinetic energy as a dissipated quantity into heat with a Kolmogorov-style loss (so every joule
is accounted: input work equals kinetic change plus dissipation). (5) BLASTS as an event law, not
CFD: a bounded-region energy release with a Friedlander overpressure pulse by scaled distance
(Kingery and Bulmash, TNT equivalence; Baker et al., Explosion Hazards and Evaluation; (k))
feeding the existing impact, fracture and body laws, in the spirit of the map's rule that
resolving shocks at 5 cm is not affordable.
Cost and risk: (1) is a scalar per region and one extra term in the PCG right-hand side
(under 0.1 ms); (2), (3) are one table and a few multiplies; (4) bookkeeping only; (5) per
event, zero per tick. The risks are the legacy-stand-in diffs (declared) and correctness of the
leak model, which needs openings from the door and mechanism work (W15 batch 2).
Status: NEW. The map's "sealed room builds no pressure" row in the old roadmap (voxel-roadmap.md
line 34) was never booked into a wave. Priority: (1) high, (2)-(4) medium, (5) medium-high for
bomb scenes. Owner-level: (5) is scope and play (does the world have explosives? at what
realism); the rest is technical.

B9. COMBUSTION IS A SURFACE FLUX; THERE IS NO FUEL VAPOUR
Choice: `_burn_cells` (heat.py:2019): a solid that flames (PYRO) sits at its ignition temperature
at the face and converts solid to gas at L_g per gram, up to q_fl and a per-material rate, with a
linear oxygen factor from 25 percent above the limit down to the limit (`_o2_factor`); the
flame temperature is a closed form of heat per gram of O2 and a mixing constant LAMBDA_FREE 1.4
(materials.py:615, "CALIBRATED, not measured"); the flame is a cluster of cells labelled by the
world law, with the Heskestad length (F25); the "pyrolysed fuel gas" is not a species, it heats
cells at once. One ignition temperature per material, no pilot, no autoignition; oil is a pool
that burns at its top, ign 250 C, 60 g/m2s (FLUID OIL row). (v)
Why: a flux law gives a fire of the right size cheaply with no chemistry, and the world had no
gas species field (SMOKE was "all visible products").
Realism cost: (a) NO FLASH POINT OR FLAMMABILITY LIMIT: spirit (ethanol flash point 13 C), lamp oil
(flash about 50 to 90 C), tallow, wine, brandy (132 wine and brandy cases in the map) and any
liquid fuel light by one ignition number; a pool of ether or spirit in a warm room should give
a flash fire above the pool, but here lights only where the SURFACE hits a threshold; (b) no flammable
GAS or VAPOUR: methane, hydrogen, spirit vapour in a cellar, flour dust, a closed lamp-oil room;
no premixed flame, no explosion limit, no backdraft (no unburnt fuel gas to accumulate and meet
air at a door), no rollover or flashover as a gas-phase event: the map says these "emerge from gas
velocity plus radiation", but they cannot emerge while the pyrolysis gas is booked as heat at the
face; (c) piloted ignition and autoignition are one number, so a sunlit dry plank at 300 C lights
without a flame; the real piloted number for wood is about 350 C and autoignition 450 to 500 C
(Drysdale, Introduction to Fire Dynamics; Babrauskas, Ignition Handbook; (k)); (d) no
extinction by dilution (CO2, steam), only by oxygen; a steam-filled room does not smother; (e)
no cooling of a fire by thick wet fuel (moisture) beyond the new two-pool wood water (14a R3).
Alternatives: ONE GAS-PHASE PATH, the one FDS uses (McGrattan; mixture fraction with a
single-step, infinitely fast, mixing-limited reaction and the eddy-dissipation concept of
Magnussen and Hjertager; (k)): (1) pyrolysis puts FUEL VAPOUR grams in the gas store (gasstore.py
already holds named species; v) instead of heat; (2) a cell burns the vapour where fuel, oxygen
and a temperature above a species-specific ignition threshold (or a pilot flame in a neighbour)
meet, within flammability limits from a species row; the heat released is booked per gram of
O2 (the 13.1 MJ per kg O2 rule the code already uses; v, materials.py O2_RATIO comment); (3)
liquids: evaporation by the existing psychro surface law, so the FLASH POINT is simply where the
vapour concentration reaches the lower flammability limit (Antoine vapour pressure, a species
row); this replaces the oil "ignition temperature" and gives spirits for free; (4) the
flame labelling and Heskestad height become a CHECK on the emergent flame (a test), not a law,
which removes the double-count risk of correlation inside a flow solver; (5) piloted ignition by
a flame or spark neighbour at a lower threshold, autoignition higher, per material (a column);
(6) the same species path gives dust (a fine fuel suspended: LFL by concentration), methane and
hydrogen, alcohol vapour, and the magic waste "motes and byproducts" (decision Q1) as gas species.
Cost and risk: one more gas scalar per fuel species (about 6 ms a scalar today per the map, much
less once B7 and the lazy gas store hold only active cells and numba or Rust carry the
advection); the reaction is a local min of fuel and oxygen per cell per substep (cheap). Risk:
the flame has to burn in the 20 cm gas cell, so the flame's real thickness (millimetres) is
sub-cell: use the mixing-limited rate with the cell as the mixing scale (as FDS does at coarse
resolution) and verify with the same acceptance tests (Heskestad length, McCaffrey plume, F-T9
crib) the map uses. Real-time risk medium: the vapour scalar is only active near a fire.
Status: NEW (the map lists "Smouldering, CO as its own gas" and "Ventilation, flashover" at P2 and
"fine fuels and igniters" at P1; none is in a decided wave). Priority: high. Owner-level: YES in
scope: how deep combustion goes (surface flux only, versus fuel vapour and explosions) is a trade
of realism, scope and cost; recommend the single gas-phase path because it ALSO unlocks B13 and
the explosive and magic-byproduct scenes and deletes stand-ins.

B12. CHAR, SMOULDER, EMBERS AND REKINDLING

B13. SMOKE IS ONE SCALAR, AND THE SOOT, CO AND TOXICITY HOOKS STAY ON OLD NUMBERS

same kernel pass (one limiter pass for n species), which is how FDS keeps species cost low (k).
Status: 14a R1 (smoke sight depends on light) and the data hooks exist; the species and the
unwiring are NEW (map F19 P1 "after gas", Purser S). Priority: high (smoke is the main killer in
fire scenes and is what the sight, hearing and escape decisions run on). Owner-level: no.

B25. THE GAS GRID IS FIXED AT 20 CM (4 VOXELS); FLAME CELLS ARE 5 CM

B15. LIQUIDS ARE A CELL AUTOMATON: NO FLOW RATE, NO MOMENTUM, NO VISCOSITY
Choice: each cell holds a volume (fvol, ml) of one liquid; `_law_flow` and `_law_head` (fluids.py:
1014, 1120) sweep wet cells lowest first and level neighbours down to LEVEL_TOL_M 2e-5; heavier
over lighter swaps (the oil-fire "water slips under" rule); the sweep order, not a velocity, sets the
speed; drops fly as ballistic parcels (deterministic, `_fly_drops`); SPLASH_UP 1.1 and SPLASH_SIDE 1.6
m/s are "UNVERIFIED"; molten tin, lead and iron "run like water" (materials.py:126 comment). (v)
Why: the map: "3-D water costs 10 to 1000x more"; the cellular rule is exact, books mass, and a still
pond can sleep (fluids.py header).
Realism cost: (a) there is no speed of flow: a spill levels in one sweep so a flood front has no
travel time; a narrow pipe does not throttle (map: "No momentum. A narrow pipe does not throttle");
a river has no current, so it cannot carry a body, a boat, a log or a sheet, or sweep a man off his
feet (map "Standing in a current"); (b) no waves or sloshing: a bucket carried by a walking man
does not slosh, a ship does not roll, a pond does not ripple; (c) viscosity is absent entirely, so
honey, tar, oil and a molten metal pour as fast as water and a thick stream does not coil; (d)
no hydraulic jump, no weir, no lock; flood and dam scenes have no dynamics; (e) buoyancy and drag on
things are cell-wise (map "Buoyancy and drag on things"); (f) boats and swimming (256 boat cases;
cold-water, drowning) are impossible.
Alternatives: (1) THE SHALLOW-WATER (VIRTUAL PIPES) HEIGHT FIELD for open water (Mei, Decaudin and
Hu 2007; O'Brien and Hodgins 1995; Saint-Venant; (k)): one height and flux per column pair, volume
exact, speed from g and depth, a current to read; the map measured 0.026 to 2.5 ms for 2 to 20 m
(v, realism-map 3.2); couple to the lattice at its edges and to the drops. (2) A per-liquid
viscosity (Poiseuille limit on a flow) as a cap on the per-tick cell flux, so oil and tar run slowly
and lava crawls; Torricelli outflow for vessels (map). (3) For the splash and pour: MPM already
exists in src/mpm and is used for soil and sand (v, repo layout); use it for BOUNDED splash events
(a short burst of particles, then demote back to cells, as the bonds and topple code does for
rigid bodies) instead of tabled speeds, so a thrown bucket and a diving man splash from the
impact energy. (4) A sloshing mode: one lumped mass-spring pendulum per vessel (Ibrahim,
Liquid Sloshing Dynamics; (k)) rather than a fluid, for carried vessels.
Cost: (1) amortised tiny and only where water is large; (2) free; (3) event-bound, tens of ms for
a burst (u); (4) microseconds. Risks: the seam between height-field, cell liquid and drop parcels
(conservation tests exist for drops, v); keep exact books at each seam.
Status: NEW (map P2 "Open water as a height field", "Buoyancy and drag", "A person in water",
"Standing in a current"; none is in a decided wave). Priority: high for any water, boat or flood
scene; medium otherwise. Owner-level: YES in scope: does the world include rivers, boats, swimming
and floods? Recommend (1) plus (2) first (cheap, exact) and (3) only when a scene needs it.

the solid's surface energy; capillary length per liquid exists); sorptivity per porous material

Status: NEW. Priority: low. Owner: no.

B17. LIQUID PROPERTIES ARE CONSTANTS, AND THERE ARE EIGHT LIQUIDS

B21. LIGHT DOES NOT EXIST
Choice: sight checks only what blocks the view (optical depth: SEE_TAU 2, SEE_SMOKE_TAU 3,
SEE_LIT_SMOKE 3/8); light is a single lux number read from the climate's sun for smoke sight, and
a reference 300 lx otherwise (senses.py _ambient_lux: "The sim has no light field ... Flame and lamp
light are not summed in (open item)"); marks.py has pure photometric functions
(lux_from_source, luminance, legible) not wired to a field. (v)
Why: sight was built before heat; light needs a field and rays; the map put it at P1 and L effort.
Realism cost: every place is lit: a cellar, a cave, a night street, a shuttered room reveal
everything; torches and lanterns do nothing for sight; stealth by darkness, hiding in shadow, a
dark-adapted watchman, reading by a candle (marks.legible takes E_lux but is never fed one),
being blinded by a flash, the glow of a forge through a door, seeing a lit window from far away,
moonlit versus overcast nights, firelight on faces, colour of firelight, and the whole class
of "haunted dim room" cues the coverage work already identified (decisions: dim featureless rooms
plus suggestibility) are not representable. Day also does nothing: the sun lights no room.
Alternatives: (1) LIGHT AS A FIELD ON THE SAME PATCH SYSTEM AS B2 (shortwave band): direct light from
point and area sources (E = I cos theta / d^2 times transmittance by the existing ray walker, v
heat.py _throw is the pattern), the sun and sky as boundary sources (sky view exists, v outside.py
PSI_OUT and NDIR), indirect by the radiosity solve or by the cheap "lumen method" per room
(IES Lighting Handbook; (k)); flood-fill propagation volumes are the cheap game alternative
(Kaplanyan and Dachsbacher 2010, Light Propagation Volumes; the voxel-game block-light
flood fill; (k)) and are acceptable where energy booking is not needed, but light is a sensing
field, not a conserved quantity of the sim, so an approximate field with exact SIGNALS (lux at
the eye) is the right trade. (2) PHOTOMETRY: flame, lamp and candle in lumens from the watts burning
(luminous efficacy of a flame, the map's 0.16 to 0.3 lm/W for a candle; (v) as claimed in
realism-map 3.5, (k) otherwise), an incandescent hot surface from Planck's law times the eye
response (a 1000 C bar is orange and bright: the colour module heathist_colour.py already has the
glow colour; v); lux to the eye as the sight input. (3) SPECTRAL BANDS: 3 to 4 bands so firelight is
orange, moonlight blue-grey, magic light any colour (decision Q1: waste as light, a spectrum,
so a COLOUR), glass and cloth selective (B2's two-band absorptance becomes per-band). (4) Mirrors
and windows are transmittance and one specular term (B2).
Cost and risk: the map's estimate is 0.15 to 0.25 ms a source on a 20 cm grid, cached on change,
about 1 to 3 ms a tick amortised (v quoting the map; u as a measurement); the patch system is
shared with B2 so the incremental cost is the source list and the sight integral. Risks: a
second field in every room (use the lazy store: dark means zero); the sight law must be switched
from optical depth to contrast (B22) at the same time or the field has no consumer.
Status: NEW (map P1, marked "(owner)", not in any decided wave; 14a R1 only reads the sun). Recommend
slotting it before 16b (knowledge uses sight as a channel: "sight models" in K3 and K4) and before
magic light. Priority: high. Owner-level: YES: the bands (3 or 4 vs RGB), how dark is dark (the
play effect of true night), and whether the sun is a light AND a heat source (recommended, B2).

B22. SIGHT IS A BINARY OPTICAL-DEPTH CUT, NOT A CONTRAST AND ACUITY LAW
Choice: a look penetrates optical depth SEE_TAU 2 for solids and foliage and 3 for smoke; a flame
is seen to 8; no contrast, no acuity, no adaptation, no colour, no glare, no distance limit by
size; the belief sweep ignores smoke and darkness (smoke.belief_ignores_smoke, v).
Cost: a face is "seen" or not by geometry; recognising a face at 20 m in twilight, reading a
sign across a room, spotting a dark figure against a dark wall, being dazzled by a lantern, night
adaptation (rods, 20 to 30 min; Hecht), peripheral detection, camouflage and motion detection all
are missing; the "detecting versus identifying" row of the map (Blackwell 1946 contrast threshold;
a face identified at about 0.8 lux at 4 m) is unbuilt (v, map).
Alternative: contrast threshold detection (Blackwell; CIE and the Adrian visibility model; (k)) from
the luminance of target and background (B21), acuity by visual angle and luminance (Snellen,
Hecht), adaptation level as a state per person (a dial plus history, matching the owner's
"traits = dials" rule), glare by CIE disability glare (Holladay-Stiles; (k)), and the smoke path
as a contrast loss by the soot law (already V = C/K, Jin; v).
Cost: microseconds per look; the rays exist. Status: NEW (map P1 and P2); depends on B21.
Priority: high with B21. Owner-level: no (the law is standard); the dial ranges are tuned.

B23. SOUND: ONE BAND, ONE STRAIGHT-LINE LEVEL, A STATIC AMBIENT, NO ROOM ACOUSTICS
Choice (v, senses.py `_level`, materials SOUND dict, policy LOMBARD): each sound is a level in dB
at 1 m; the level at an ear is 20 log10 distance, minus the mass-law loss of the walls along the
straight line (total mass, applied once), gaps by energy, a body 3 dB, foliage by ISO 9613-2, and
the louder of that and a way round a corner with the ISO barrier bending cap of 20 dB; the
wavelength is fixed at 500 Hz (wave_m 0.686); words are understood if the level beats HEAR_DB 40
and the ambient minus 3 dB; the ambient is ONE number per world (`w.ambient_db`, default 35),
set by a scene, not computed from sources; sounds live a couple of ticks; there is no speed of
sound; the Lombard reflex exists (LOMBARD from 45 dB, slope 0.5, cap 100).
Realism cost: (a) a wall's mass law is per band: a door passes bass and stops treble, so a
muffled voice through a wall (low frequencies pass; consonants lost) and the intelligibility of
speech (SII over 6 or 7 bands) cannot be modelled; the map's "partial understanding" (a voice but no
words) needs it; (b) ambient noise is whatever the scene says: a loud fire, a crowd, a rain or a
river or a forge do not mask speech unless the author sets the number; they should come from the
sources actually running (sound power sums of every live source at the listener, with a floor);
(c) no reverberation: a stone hall, a cave, a cathedral, a tiled bath and a padded room sound
alike; speech intelligibility in a reverberant room falls with the reverberation time
(Sabine, Eyring; Kuttruff, Room Acoustics; Hodgson; (k)); (d) air absorption (ISO 9613-1: 0.1
to 3 dB per 100 m by frequency and humidity), ground effect, wind and temperature gradient
refraction (sound carries downwind and over water at night) are absent at distances over 30 m,
which matters for a shout across a field and a bell across a town; (e) no speed of sound: echo,
thunder and a distant gunshot have no delay (343 m/s: 3 s per km; the tick is 25 ms so a 8.6 m
travel is 1 tick: time of arrival is cheap to add); (f) voices are omni: a shout in front of
the speaker is 6 dB louder than behind (directivity, Chu and Warnock; (k)); (g) impact
sound (footsteps on floors above) transmits as structure-borne, not as the air mass law; (h)
sound events end after two ticks, so a droning fire, a river, a forge's hammer, a crowd's murmur
and a bell are not sustained sources (map: "Fire is silent; sounds live 2 ticks").
Alternatives: (1) OCTAVE BANDS (125 Hz to 4 kHz, 6 bands) in the existing numba ear-pair kernel,
per-band mass-law TL from the surface mass and a per-material coincidence dip (Sharp; the mass
law is the standard first-order form, (k)), per-band source spectra by class (speech, impact,
fire, water); (2) the world's AMBIENT as the energy sum of the live sustained sources per air
region at the listener, with a floor of the quiet room; (3) a Sabine or Eyring reverberant field
per airspace region (one number per region and band from volume and absorption; regions exist,
v air.py regions()), added as a diffuse level and a decay time (T60 = 0.161 V / A); (4) air
absorption by ISO 9613-1 per band from temperature and humidity (the world has both); wind
as a speed-of-sound vector offset along the path for far listeners; (5) an arrival delay by distance
over c, as a tick count, for echoes and thunder; (6) simple voice directivity by angle; (7) sound
power from energy: B24.
Cost and risk: bands multiply the ear-pair kernel by 6 (the map's batch estimate about 1 to 2 ms for

B24. SOUND SOURCE LEVELS ARE NAMED GUESSES, UNBOOKED
Choice: an impact's level is a "ring" fraction times the blow energy over 50 ms, 8 dB below a
half-space power level, "named guesses, pinned by two anchors" (a footstep about 60 dB at 1 m, a
pane on stone about 100); a fire is silent; flow, water and the gas jet make no noise (materials.py
SOUND comments). (v)
Realism cost: a bellows, a fan, a boiling pot, a pouring stream, a sword on a shield, a fire's roar
(sound power scales with heat release; Strasberg; Rajaram and Lieuwen; (k)), a river and wind in
leaves have no source; the level of an impact does not follow the energy and materials law
exactly; the energy radiated is not booked (tiny, but "every joule booked" asks the share to be
a named loss to heat).
Alternative: sound power as a book entry: radiated acoustic power equals the energy of the event
times a per-pair radiation efficiency (a small table) with the share moved to heat as a booked loss;
aeroacoustic sources from the flow (Lighthill: power scales with u^8 for a jet) and combustion
noise from the heat release rate (a standard efficiency of order 1e-6 to 1e-5; Rajaram and
Lieuwen, (k), (u) the constant).
Cost: tiny; fits with B23. Status: NEW. Priority: low. Owner-level: no.
