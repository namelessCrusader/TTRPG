LANE: CRITIC (2026-11-19). Read-only. Checked the five lane reports against the design rules.
(v) = checked by web search this run. (u) = unverified. Fix = what I would change.

VERDICT
1. The reports are good and mostly consistent. The core law (M is a finite, out-of-equilibrium store; patterns steer; every conversion is booked) holds.
2. ONE BIG HOLE: decision 8.2 (waste and recoil go to the holder) plus the biology numbers means every field-powered fireball kills its caster (A1).
3. Three "general" mechanisms hide per-case data: the pattern tag, the hand-made spell-element map, and sympathetic links (B1-B3).
4. Archetypes marked "OK" that need an extra piece: scrying, links, mind magic, spirits, levitation, shapeshift (D).
5. Number errors: Frank-Kamenetskii constants swapped in two lanes, three arithmetic slips in chemistry, one eel figure unconfirmed (E).

A. FREE ENERGY AND UNBOOKED JOULES
A1. Waste heat kills the caster. Physics P9 books heat "at the transducer (caster body or object)". Biology: the body absorbs about 1.2 MJ (5 K x 245 kJ/K) before organ damage. A 0.9 GJ fireball at 99% efficiency still wastes 9 MJ, 7 times the lethal dose. Real devices heat where energy is converted, not where the switch is.
 Fix: two booked lines. (1) Control overhead (bits x cost, plus the medium's body power in W) goes to the holder. (2) Conversion waste (1 - eta) x E_delivered appears at the SITE where M is absorbed (effect region, or the crystal on release). A pack-set share may go to the holder (8.2 option). Body-only magic keeps the 1.2 MJ cap, because the body is the store.
A2. Recoil momentum is p = E/c_M. At light speed, 0.9 GJ gives 3 kg m/s (nothing). At c_M = 100 m/s it gives 9e6 (fatal). Fix: state that recoil depends on c_M. With a fast c_M, the holder's pain is A1 heat and control cost, not momentum.
A3. M refill is booked two ways. Physics: external source S (ley, sun). Biology: M = eta x plant production. Fix: one ledger line "imported_M" with an origin class (planet, sun, life, patron, other world) and a pack rate map. Life-derived M is a real conversion and removes biomass (biology is right). Print daily import in every run so leaks show.
A4. Interference: physics P16 sums amplitudes "for declared coherent sources". A point sum with no book of the missing energy breaks the ledger. Fix: compute energy per cell over the overlap (I1 + I2 + 2 sqrt(I1 I2) cos) and book the total; charge the canceller's source its work.
A5. Slots, daily rest-refill, feruchemy (fiction A33): a refill from nothing is free energy. Fix: refill = food intake (glycogen about 7 MJ, 24 h) or local M draw. A "slot" is only a UI block of joules.
A6. Charging crystals: the lanes cover discharge and leak, not charge. Fix: charging is the transducer law run backwards (eta below 1, heat at the crystal). Density cap per material class (gem at about 1e5 MJ/kg); over-driving cracks or melts the store.
A7. Luck (A29): lawful only if the sim really has sensitive dependence. Fix: nudge physical metastable things only (a spark, a balance); never bias the RNG.

B. SPECIAL CASES HIDING IN "GENERAL" MECHANISMS
B1. Physics 6.8: "data tag per structure class giving g and B". A per-class tag is a trait flag. Fix: g from measurable properties. Materials: purity, lattice order, dopant (chemistry s.4). People and beasts: the individual dial with history and state. B from channel count and S/N (Shannon).
B2. Engineering's element space "fire near light and heat, far from ice" is hand-made per-spell data. Fix: grammar leaves are physical numbers (heat, light, kinetic fractions, radius, duration, strength); distance is distance in that space; a neighbour error is a physical neighbour. "Fire" is only a name for a region in a pack's naming table.
B3. Sympathetic links, arcane connection, true names as "a number: share of energy reaching the target" is a free nonlocal channel. Fix: the target is a resonator (f0, Q). A caster who has a copy or measurement of its pattern (hair, blood, name) broadcasts at f0 through M at c_M. Only a matched resonator absorbs strongly (physics P5). Fidelity = pattern overlap x path transmission. A true name is a perfect measurement.
B4. Ars Magica's 5 verbs x 10 forms, Mage's 9 spheres: names only. Verbs are grammar node types (release, sense, transform, disperse, move); forms are regions of the material/field table; Vim = Form is M.
B5. Allomancy "16 metals, one effect each": effect from the metal's transducer row (chemistry M4), fuelled by metabolism. No 16 code paths.
B6. Hemalurgy, "moving a trait": move a copied slice of a dial value with loss, held by a physical spike. No flags.
B7. Possession (biology P11): another mind's act channel writes to the body's motor interface, paid from host metabolism. No new mechanism.
Rule for packs: numbers only (rates, densities, c_M, g distribution). A pack may omit a medium row, but may not switch on a behaviour.

C. CONTRADICTIONS BETWEEN LANES
C1. Speed: physics c_M 1 m/s to near-instant (at least one cell per tick); engineering delivery "about zero flight time"; fiction "light-fast". Fix: one parameter, arrival tick = ceil(path / (c_M dt)), minimum 1. Aim lag, homing, scrying, counterspell races all read it.
C2. Complexity cost has three laws (physics overhead per bit and diffraction; engineering bits/rate; biology skill and fatigue). Fix: keep engineering's: cost = bits, time = bits / medium rate, joules = medium body power x time. Drop physics P10. Keep diffraction only as the aim limit.
C3. Physics P1 has M as a density AND a fast wave "on top": two stores of one energy. Fix: a pulse draws its energy from M (or crystal) at emission and returns the unabsorbed rest at the end. Book it as a transfer between named stores.
C4. Heredity: biology says child = mid-parent + noise (h2 = 0.5, Polderman 2015, 49% average (v)); fiction wants born casters. Additive models rarely make extremes. Fix: add a small chance of a large jump in g (still a dial).
C5. Patron channel: physics "link with bandwidth", engineering "remote server with latency and secret", biology "market with rate and sanctions". They agree. One record: {bandwidth, latency = path/c_M, exchange rate, sanction}.
C6. Body-only magic: biology gives about 300 W chemical sustained (fat, 0.5 g/min), about 30 kJ burst (ATP + phosphocreatine), 7 MJ glycogen, 1.2 MJ heat cap. Chemistry: a fireball is 25 kg of fat. So body-only is a thrown-rock scale, not an attack spell. Lawful, but the user should see it.

D. LOCALITY AND ARCHETYPES THE PIECES CANNOT YET BUILD
D1. Scrying (A26, "OK, light-fast"): sensing at range needs something at the far end. Without it, it is a nonlocal read. Fix: M wave out and echo back (1/r^4 like bats, biology 1.6), or a linked resonator at the far end (B3). Shielding then blocks it.
D2. Spirits, gods, golem wills (A11, A22): a dissipative pattern needs intake, boundary, repair, but a scalar M density holds no information; a mind needs bits. Fix (decision Q4): either minds always have a substrate (body, gem, stored pattern with a bit capacity per volume) or spirits are reflex-only dissipative structures.
D3. Mind magic (A20) "OK given an M-neuron coupling": the sim has no neurons. Fix: a general "neural bias" effect form moving dials the sim already has (fear, trust, attention, mood); tiny energy, strength from g and S/N, resisted by target noise. Reading minds is the same coupling with about 10 bits/s capacity (Zheng and Meister 2024).
D4. Levitation, flight, telekinesis: a force needs a reaction. Physics gives M momentum, so the reaction goes to the field or ground. Fix: every force effect names its reaction partner in the ledger. Hover costs only the conversion loss.
D5. Shapeshift (A23) "70 kg to 70 kg is OK" ignores rearrangement energy (biology: about 5-10 MJ per kg regrown) and the 1.2 MJ heat cap. A full change is hundreds of MJ. Fix: slow (days, heat shed over time) or heat dumped to a store. Size change needs mass (N3).
D6. Summoning "fetch a real being by fast motion" is a kinetic launch, not a summon. Remove it from the build list.
D7. Golems and animated objects: need sensor, controller, actuator and a power budget (engineering s.3-4). Not a free will.
D8. Elemental bending (A19) "local fire as source": a campfire gives little work. Fix: bending steers matter already moving, with M force; the local element supplies mass.
D9. Teleport and time (N1, N2): flagged correctly. Cheapest honest change: a booked "relocate" with a pack switch and an ordering rule (applies at end of tick).
D10. Missing archetypes: weather control; plant growth (mass from soil and water); buffs (ATP and heat limits); invisibility (narrow-band transformation optics, physics s.7); life drain (heat engine at body temperature is 3% efficient, so it is chemical transfer); replicating curses and disease (lycanthropy, vampirism = physics P12 + biology parasite law); disenchanting (induced currents burn traces); sound and illusion; long-range comms (Shannon C on an M channel).
D11. Missing interactions: two casters on one M cell (brown-out); M leak heats and glows the place; engravings sharing f0 (beats, detuning); magic on combustion (a spark is a nucleus); magic on electronics (induced currents); over-harvest of M-rich ecosystems; anti-magic zones and bodies that need coupling; a catalyst-poisoned stone (chemistry s.7); a patron cutting a channel mid-cast. Add an interaction matrix to the synthesis: store x store, wave x wave, wave x material, wave x mind, wave x life, wave x fire.

2. Frank-Kamenetskii values (Gray, Boddington, Robinson 1979): slab 0.88, infinite cylinder 2, sphere 3.32 (v). Physics says "sphere 2.0, cylinder 3.32"; chemistry says "sphere 2.0". Swapped. Fix both.
3. De Santana et al. 2019, Nat Commun 10:4000: 860 V confirmed (v); no ampere figure found. Biology's "1 A" and "1.7 J per pulse" are (u). Only six fish were measured.
4. Toyabe 2010 (Nat Phys 6:988): 28% is free energy gained over mutual information (v); physics wrote "28% of kT ln2", a loose paraphrase. Koski 2014 (PNAS 111:13786): up to 75% of kT ln2 per cycle (v). Different ratios; do not compare.
5. Gallant 2014 (Science 344:1522): at least six lineages (v). The "scn4aa second copy down in muscle, up in organ" detail is only in secondary summaries; downgrade biology's (v) to (u).
6. Coupe 2019 (Sci Adv): 17 languages, about 39 bits/s (v). Zheng and Meister 2024 (10 bits/s) not returned by my search; I know the paper, treat as (k).

Lead to gold, 38 GJ/g: I recomputed from atomic masses (about 79 MeV per atom, 3.8e10 J/g), correct. Landauer 2.87e-21 J, 3e29 bits per 0.9 GJ, body 245 kJ/K: correct.
The fiction lane did no web checks (it says so). Its list facts (16 allomantic metals, Ars Magica 5 techniques and 10 forms, 9 Mage spheres) match what I know. "Slippage" and "hemalurgy loss" numbers are (u); keep as hints.

F. CHEAPER ESTIMATING LAWS
1. M field lazy: unused cells relax as M = S/k + (M0 - S/k) exp(-kt); store (M0, t0), compute on read; diffuse only near recent draws.
2. Pulses: events with arrival time and a transmission product (P16). Keep.
3. Metastable release: one time-to-event from the barrier law; check critical size once per heat change, not per tick.
4. Neighbour error: precomputed discrete kernel; block failure is a binomial tail.
5. Practice x(n) = x1 n^-b, closed form. Wear: Miner's rule per use.
6. Body heat: one scalar core temperature ODE per caster.
7. Spirits: scalar ODE for M_self, wake on intake change. Ecosystem M map: daily refresh.
8. Potions: one-compartment closed form. Choir: e/sqrt(n), not n casters. Healing: day-scale rate law with mass from food.
Never estimate: the energy ledger, the heat cap, M conservation.
