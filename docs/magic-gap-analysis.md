# The gap analysis: how much magic existing physics already allows, and the least we must add

Started with the owner on 2026-11-27. It answers two issues the owner raised:
1. We cannot derive real physics bottom-up, so how do we add magic MINIMALLY?
2. We want to know how much magic is already possible in existing physics, change the least to make the rest possible, and still get many kinds of magic to emerge.

## The answer to issue 1: minimal at the effective level

At everyday energies and sizes the known laws are complete and well tested (Carroll, arXiv:2101.07884). Physicists judge a proposed extension by what it ADDS to that effective description: new fields, new charges, new interaction terms and new free numbers (a dark photon is one new field plus one mixing number). MARK1 measures its own minimality the same way. The bottom-up research (the emergent medium, FQ1 and RQ1-RQ4) does not have to be true of our universe; it has to be a CONSISTENT GENERATOR of the few additions. Minimal at the top, consistent at the bottom.

## The method for issue 2

1. **The wanted magic.** The case bank (docs/magic-science.md section 4) and the tropes catalogue (docs/coverage/merged.tsv, 4,128 rows).
2. **Sort every item into a tier** (the vocabulary follows Kaku's classes of "impossibilities", Physics of the Impossible, 2008):
   - **T0, ordinary physics, no magic needed:** bodies, animals, chemistry, crafts, social and mental phenomena.
   - **T1, ordinary physics STEERED:** possible with existing physics given only control, information and energy delivered at a distance. This is what the base addition supplies (A1 below).
   - **T2, needs a further addition** from the candidate list below.
   - **T3, needs a broken law:** creation from nothing, faster-than-light signalling, travel to the past, perpetual motion, true omniscience. These are packs and doorways (A6, the axioms, Q10).
   - **X, not a physical effect:** a tool, a meta-feature or pure narrative.
3. **The candidate additions** (each a named change to physics, in rough order of size):
   - **A1 the base:** one gauge field with electric magic charge, carried by organised matter, touching ordinary physics through the three portals (binding, mixing, energy exchange). Control, sensing and energy at a distance.
   - **A2 magnetic charge and dyons:** strings (confinement), oblique regions, magnetoelectric effects (RQ4).
   - **A3 condition-dependent charge-2 pairing:** sharp-bordered dead zones, half-quantum threads, sensing at a distance (Aharonov-Bohm), pinning and steering of threads (RQ4).
   - **A4 a non-abelian symmetry:** stable field knots as particles, braiding memory (RQ4's rival hypothesis).
   - **A5 field self-interaction:** self-sustaining packets, a density-dependent index, field states (R8, MS2, Q1.1 (c)).
   - **A6 lasting imprints:** the field remembers (R5).
   - **A7 a strong mind-field coupling:** the mind partly made of the field: ghosts, possession, minds touching directly (Q2.4 (c)).
   - **A8 a doorway:** a flux-carrying join between places (Q10), booked energy and momentum.
   - **other:** anything a row needs that is not on this list (each is a finding).
4. **Choose the minimal set:** a coverage matrix of wanted items against additions; the smallest set of additions that covers the T1 and T2 items (a set cover), with each addition's cost (new fields, charges, terms and numbers).
5. **Measure emergent variety:** how many DISTINCT kinds of effect each addition unlocks (by families and mechanisms in the catalogue now, and later by what the toy simulations actually produce through composition). A good addition is one small change that opens many kinds of magic.
