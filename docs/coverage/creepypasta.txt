WHAT WAS SAMPLED, AND WHY
278 of roughly 12,000 seen titles. The Fandom wiki is dominated by thousands of one-off user stories; I took every FAMILY (a trope, such as mirror ritual, parasite host, cursed cartridge, rule list) with named representatives, and every Wikipedia-notable item in the creepypasta, analog horror, backrooms, ARG, Japanese-legend and Fortean categories I saw. Within the huge Fandom thematic categories (Mirrors 166, Ghosts 840, Demons 604) one row stands for a trope, not each story. Most single-story entries are the same few mechanisms again. Mechanism, verdicts and fear notes are mine and are a first pass; check before building packs.

TOP MISSING LAWS (general, small)
1. In-world computation substrate: a game, console or VM that can host an entity or a mind (BEN Drowned, Sonic.exe, Knob.exe, VR experiment, backlogs). 4 LAW rows.
2. Procedural unbounded plane generator with inconsistent adjacency (the Backrooms, levels, The End). X gives doorways and planes, not endless generated layouts. 3 rows.
3. Perception-triggered mind-state hook: content that changes the receiver on being perceived or understood (Kunekune, memetic symbol, dream entities, Smile Dog). 4 rows incl. combined.
4. Recording/broadcast medium with corruption and an embedded signal (Local 58, Petscop, Marble Hornets). Media as a physical channel that carries hidden patterns. 3 rows.
5. Knowledge-gated curse spread: the curse is carried by having been told or having seen (Red Room, Smile Dog, chain letters). Also fits a general "contagion by information".
6. Rule-bound agents: conditional compliance triggers (Rule Horror, Bloody Mary, Kuchisake-onna, black-eyed children, Midnight game). Listed as a PACK dependency in about 12 rows: a pattern or entity whose action is keyed to what an observer does or answers.
7. SECOND-change: time loops, changing the past, true omnipotence or reality rewrite (4 rows).

WHAT IS SURPRISING
- About half of the legends are COVERED by K and S alone: rumour mutation, shared false memory (Candle Cove, Mandela effect), misperception, moral panic. Creepypasta is mostly a rumour-and-perception genre, so MARK1's strongest layer fits best.
- Almost no entry needs a second change. Only time-based stories do. Time loops are the genre's most common need that MARK1 refuses.
- Ghosts, curses, possession, rituals, haunted objects are all already M designs (pattern, store, link, drain). The new work is data packs, not law.
- Observer-conditioned entities (the SCP-173 type) were expected to need a law but a pattern that senses gaze through M is arguably a pack. Left as PACK, flagged for design (u).
- The hardest fear to deliver is not a law but the narrator: scenes that work by withholding (liminal spaces, analog horror) depend on K and the narrator showing only what a character perceives.
- Backrooms need exactly one law (generator) and then are only packs: each level is a plane pack.
- 5 EXCLUDED rows are meta (aesthetics, Zalgo glyph text, product pages, simulation claim, water-fuelled car).
