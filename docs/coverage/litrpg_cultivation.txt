TOP MISSING LAWS (rows each; clusters approximate)
1. Bounded death-reversal: a stored body pattern is restored after death at a booked cost (respawn, resurrection, revive, nine lives, Lazarus, immortalized)  ~6, +3 more already in merged. Probably a bounded use of the X rewind; needs a rule for how the snapshot is made and kept.
2. M-driven tissue repair with a rate cap (heal spells, potions, regeneration, limb regrowth)  ~6. Healing is the most common effect in LitRPG and it is not listed as a magic effect in the design: it needs energy booked from M and a biology limit on how fast tissue can grow.
3. Acquired-sense channel that reports derived aggregate scores (HUD, status window, appraisal)  ~4. The numbers must be defined as measurable proxies, else the character perceives a number the world does not have (this is a K and S design question as much as a law).
4. Brain-state decode (soul search, detect thoughts, walk in their shoes), percept-as-payload (fear spell, reaper gaze, mind horror, charm), luck bias (karma, roll the dice): existing laws in merged, re-confirmed here.

WHERE THE FEAR AND EXCITEMENT COME FROM
- Excitement here is mostly growth and mastery (K: anticipation of the next threshold; the learning history is the engine), not dread.
- The fear specific to this lane is S and K together: an authority that grades you, an audience that watches you, patrons with their own agenda, a contract you did not choose, and killing that pays. These come from institutions and information asymmetry, not from physics.
- Body fear (sepsis, poison, dismemberment, parasites, gu worms) is plain P and G and is already covered.

SURPRISES
- The System itself (DCC's AI, the Solo Leveling gate System, the Scum Villain System) is not a law. It is an entity with sensors, an effect reach and a ledger, i.e. a pack built from existing layers: a machine mind (P,K) with patron-style bonds (M) and an audience economy (S). The expensive parts are the HUD and the death-reversal, not the System.
- Levels, classes, skills and achievements all reduce to a reaction-pack on a logged event stream (trigger: kill, location, condition; effect: dial change or verb unlock). That fits the plan "LM output always logged as typed deltas": achievements are cheap if the event log is complete. The more important design question is that "killing pays" is an incentive the pack author sets, and the learned minds will respond to it (S).
- Cultivation realms are threshold jumps in a store dial; tribulation is a threshold discharge. Both are instabilities already in the M design, so xianxia needs data, not code.
- Of the 3 SECOND rows, two (rebirth, regression) are one thing: information to the past. A bounded alternative is "memory is kept across a respawn" with no change to the world, which is law 1 above.
- Isekai and "trapped in a game" are doorways plus a rule-authority pack; the isekai trope "truck-kun" is narrative only (EXCLUDED), as are alignment and saving throws (labels and dice).
