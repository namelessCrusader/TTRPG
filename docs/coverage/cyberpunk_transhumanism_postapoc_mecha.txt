WHAT IS SURPRISING
- The big three of this lane map almost one to one onto the M design: the STALKER Zone is "a region with elevated M that grows patterned stores, anomalies and mutants". Anomalies are stationary M patterns with a trigger and a drain, artifacts are charged materials, psi mutants (Burer, Poltergeist, Controller) are M users or patron-host pairs. The whole Zone is almost pure PACK data once a region-level M concentration exists.
- Warhammer Chaos is the archetype of "beings with vast substrates of believers' linked patterns": the gods, daemons, possession, Orks (WAAAGH! as belief-powered reality) and the warp-as-plane are all COVERED with no new law. Daemon engines are patron-host in a machine.
- Cyberpunk needs one new idea more than any other: the machine substrate (rule 1) and mind copy/link rules (3 and 6). Without them the net, upload, braindance and AI-god rows (about 40) stay as props. With them cyberpunk's fear (loss of self, hijacked body, the person you left behind) comes from K and the body.
- Shadowrun's Essence is a clean example of a ledger the engine could own: replacing body costs M capacity. It also explains cyberpsychosis (drift tied to implant count) if drift is a dial.
- Fear in this lane is mostly the unknown rule (Zone, Metro anomalies: K), the body (mutation, implants), and institutions (dystopia, corporations: S). Post-apocalypse rows lean on scarcity and crowd panic; the crowd-dynamics law from the merged list matters (Left 4 Dead, 28 Days Later, Ohmu stampede).
- Mecha is the least magical: most is P engineering plus a pilot link. The drift, AT Field, Newtype and spiral energy are all expressible with M links, wards and belief substrates. Only time dilation and a jamming particle need physics laws.
