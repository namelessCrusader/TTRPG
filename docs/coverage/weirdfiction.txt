Surprises:
- Most of the Gothic canon is COVERED already: ghosts, curses, pacts, possession, doubles and vampires are the existing M designs (stores, bonds, patrons, drain) plus K (misperception and rumour). Lovecraft's lesser beings are PACK.
- What does not fit is a short list: time travel (Hounds of Tindalos, Yith, Silver Key sequels, Azathoth and Yog-Sothoth as all-time presence), which is a real SECOND, and a cluster of mind-level laws that K lacks today.
- The horror of the Mythos is mostly K (knowledge, scale, indifference). The sim cannot make that frightening by adding monsters; it needs minds that lose coherence when they learn things that do not fit.
- Gothic ambiguity (Turn of the Screw, Usher, Owl Creek, Udolpho) is nearly free, because K already separates what happens from what a character perceives.
- Excluded for content: Wandering Jew trope (bigotry), and Red Hook is kept only as a neutral culture pack.

Top missing laws (each one line):
1. Content-triggered mind transformation (forbidden knowledge, the King in Yellow, Y'golonac): a concept that cannot fit a mind's model destabilises it.
2. Persistent stress-trauma state for minds (sanity): a lasting store that changes later choices.
3. Perception-rewriting field or sense-extension (Daoloth, From Beyond, flying polyps): percept-level edits, not only noise.
4. Body-frozen mind-conscious state (Ghatanothoa, Valdemar): a body stopped while the mind keeps running.
5. Reversibility window of death and restart of a dead brain (Herbert West, Frankenstein).
6. Alien-matter contamination field (Colour Out of Space): a mutagenic, draining zone from foreign matter.
7. Mind-state-gated plane access (Dreamlands, Kadath): sleep or trance opens a link to a plane.
8. Spatially localised planar leak (The Willows): a place where the barrier thins.
9. Drug-shifted personality dials with body change (Jekyll and Hyde).
SECOND (true second change): chronal travel and all-time presence (Hounds of Tindalos, Yith, Azathoth, Yog-Sothoth); omnipotent source (Azathoth).
