Surprising:
- Most sci-fi "technology" collapses into M (momentum or energy at a distance) plus X (rebuild): force fields, tractor beams, artificial gravity, reactionless drives, inertial dampening, blasters, lightsabers, teleporters, replicators all fit with M budgets as the limiter.
- The real gap is not exotic physics but SCALE: space-based sci-fi needs orbits, radiation, sensors and a slow world tick. These are ordinary physics laws, not a second change.
- Only FTL and past-directed time are true SECOND items; wormhole doorways in X give stargates without FTL signalling.
- AI, uploads, hive minds and aliens are mostly K layer: fear comes from opaque goals, misperception and the identity question, not from technology.
- Sci-fi fear sources split: body (disgust, vacuum, radiation), K (unknown intent, paranoia about who is human), and scale (awe, insignificance).
