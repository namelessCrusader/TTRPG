WHAT IS SURPRISING:
- Most of the religion layer is already COVERED: miracles reduce to P plus K (mould in a host, psychosomatic stigmata, mummification, crowd suggestion), and the few real-power ones (levitation, healing) fit M momentum and X rewind.
- Gods are the cleanest fit to the "believer-linked substrate that weakens when faith breaks" rule: Marduk, Zeus, Huitzilopochtli (needs feeding), Tenjin (a vengeful ghost promoted to a patron), apotheosis in general. Sacrifice becomes an upkeep drain; this gives a mechanical reason for the horror in sacrifice rituals.
- Fear is mostly K: omen dread, tempter voices, being watched and judged (recording angel, Medjed), plus body horror (flesh from bread, flayed skins, petrification).
- Grimoires, sigils, circles and pacts map one-to-one onto "patterns, wards and bonds with a drain"; real-life grimoires are PACK data with no new law.
- The divination family is almost entirely a K sink: cold reading, ideomotor effects and self-fulfilling expectation. True foresight is the one SECOND item. A good design test: a diviner who is right 60 percent of the time through cold reading and rumour is believable without breaking physics.
- Real-world ritual harm (child sacrifice, albino body-part trade, child witchcraft accusations) is sensitive; entries are kept as S-layer fear sources only, for design awareness, not as content to depict. (u: how the owner wants these handled)
