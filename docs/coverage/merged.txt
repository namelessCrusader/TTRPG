WHERE THE FEAR COMES FROM, BY LAYER (keyword tally of the fear_source column; rows may count in several)
 K (unknown, misperception, rumour, unpredictability, loss of agency): ~1177 rows (about 51%). This dominates every lane. Fear lives in the gap between what a character perceives and what is true; the narrator-shows-only-perception rule is the single largest horror engine.
 Body (P/G: pain, disgust, predation, paralysis, parasites, loss of bodily control): ~517 rows (about 22%). Strongest in SCP bio, bestiaries, folklore predators.
 S (social: trusted role betrayed, taboo, institutions, reputation, cults): ~131 rows (about 6%). Mostly vampires-in-society, cults, SCP-like institutions, folklore.
 M and X as DIRECT fear sources are rare (~5 rows): M and X supply the mechanism, K and body supply the dread. Pure scale (Azathoth, cosmic) is a K-failure: no mind to model.
EXCITEMENT (superpowers, sci-fi tech) is mostly admiration and competence (K: seeing capability) rather than dread.

SURPRISES
 - Only about 11% of rows need new physics or a new law; "a second change" is ~3%. The single-change design holds up well.
 - The biggest gap is not physics at all but INFORMATION: law 1 (infohazard), 3 (mind copy/brain state) and 4 (memory edit) are all K-layer laws. The corpus wants minds to be attackable through perception and memory.
 - Orbital / planet scale is the biggest pure-physics gap and mostly comes from sci-fi; it is a scale/tick question, not a new law of nature.
 - Time is the main SECOND cluster; no lane found a way to build loops or precognition from M alone.
