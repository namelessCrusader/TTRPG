# Agreements: one commitment claim, many holders

Scoped with the owner on 2026-11-22 (decisions AG1-AG6), after magic decision MC4. The owner: some debts are "the world has given observance to this pact, now both of us are beholden to it". Many kinds of entities can enter many kinds of agreements: some have no ledger and what people remember counts, and some are more intrinsic. "Without magic things are a bit straightforward; with magic A LOT is possible."

## AG1. One structure

- An agreement is a COMMITMENT CLAIM: "A will do X; B will do Y, if Z". It is typed like any belief (docs/knowledge-design.md, K1), with the seven belief fields.
- Kinds differ ONLY IN WHO OR WHAT HOLDS THE CLAIM, and so in how a breach is noticed and answered:

| Held by | Kind | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| the parties' memories | a handshake, a promise | their concerns and their reputation (K5 track-record trust) |
| witnesses | a witnessed oath | gossip and reputation |
| a physical record | a contract, a tally stick | proof in disputes (it can be forged, lost or burned; K4) |
| an institution | a guild charter, a court order | its people acting on it (the later social layer) |
| a PATTERN in the parties, in the field, or in a being of oaths | a fae bargain, a geas, an unbreakable vow | stored energy acting on breach |

- The parties remember the terms differently, witnesses disagree, a written copy settles the dispute or gets forged, and a magical pact holds even after everyone has forgotten it.

## AG2. What counts as a breach (default)

- A pact enforces ONLY WHAT IT CAN PHYSICALLY SENSE:
  - a pattern imprinted in a person senses that person's own acts and states, so an oath binding the self is fully enforceable;
  - external events need a perceiver: a witness, a being of oaths with its own senses, or a pattern with sensing reach.
- The terms are in the CLOSED CLAIM LANGUAGE and mean exactly what they say. So LITERALISM AND LOOPHOLES emerge: "you swore not to harm him, not to let him fall". This is the mechanism of the monkey's-paw wishes too (coverage CV3).
- ALTERNATIVES are possible through packs or mods: METAPHYSICALLY LINKED PACTS, such as a pact that senses breach through a bond however far away (still a channel through the field, like the magical connections of SV2) or a broader-sensing pact given by a pack law.

## AG3. Consequences of breaking a pact

- ONLY WHAT THE STORED ENERGY CAN PHYSICALLY DO.
  - Making a pact costs energy in proportion to how hard it can strike.
  - On breach, the energy acts through ordinary laws: pain, a visible mark, a drain on reserves, a burn, a spreading curse. Death needs enough stored.
  - Pacts can be drained, dispelled or outlasted as their store leaks, at real cost.
- PRECISION IS LEVERAGE (the owner: finely directed energy can do a lot). A few joules aimed at the heart's rhythm, a vessel or a nerve, or used to TRIGGER a larger store, can kill.
  - The price of precision is INFORMATION and LINK FIDELITY (cost kinds 3 and 6): a precise pattern of that person and a high-fidelity link (a blood oath, a true-name bond).
  - So the most dangerous pacts are those made with someone who knows you well.
- Social breaches answer themselves: trust drops and grudges form; gossip damages reputation; records serve as evidence; institutions sanction through their members.

## AG4. Who can make pacts, and consent

- Any being with a mind and coupling can make pacts: people, spirits, gods through their substrates, institutions through their members.
- BINDING STRENGTH FOLLOWS REAL COUPLING (magic MC6):
  - willing, understanding acceptance binds strongest;
  - COERCION binds weakly, because a resisting mind couples poorly;
  - DECEPTION binds as strongly as the acceptance was genuine (fae trickery: read the terms).
- Socially, a coerced promise carries less weight with witnesses.

## AG5. Agreements that outlive their makers

- It depends on the HOLDER:
  - memory fades with the people, though children may carry the grudge as a belief;
  - whether heirs inherit a debt is a CULTURAL BELIEF or an INSTITUTION'S RULE, and differs by place;
  - a record lasts as long as the record;
  - a magical pattern lasts WHILE ITS STORE LASTS, and can bind a BLOODLINE through a blood link, so family curses are lawful. They weaken as the store leaks, unless something feeds them.

## AG6. When to build

- The commitment claim is a belief type in WAVE 16b's schema from the start.
- Memory, witness and record kinds come with 16b (the knowledge layer).
- Institutional kinds come with the later SOCIAL LAYER.
- Magical pacts come with MAGIC (after the first slices).
- Inherited debts come as cultural beliefs (16b); bloodline curses with magic.
