One connected world, seen at three resolutions, with one mind interface above them:

  L-top   src/mind: catalog of verbs, menus, choosers, appraisal and needs rules (A4), beliefs (K1), the Player.
          Knows no backend. Stdlib only. (Already true.)
  Backends of src/mind, one Backend protocol, one conformance suite (A11):
    FINE    src/voxel via mindlink.py        the truth; 25 ms tick; bodies, matter, light, fire

  lab/: experiments only. It imports src.mind and src.rooms (one way; A3), never the reverse. It keeps its own venv for torch and its own tournaments and reports.

Where each kind of fact lives (the rule that stops the four-world drift):
  - Physical facts (matter, heat, blood, food as kcal): voxel is the truth. The coarse world keeps them as booked quantities in SI-based units with a stated error budget against voxel runs (A5).
  - Appraisals and relations (liking, trust, anger, reputation, concerns, ownership CLAIMS): in the mind, as beliefs fed by percepts (A4, A13). Not fields on a person in the world.
  - Content (cast, things, kinds, supplies): data packs read by both worlds (A6).
  - Verbs: one catalog; each backend declares which it runs; the parity table is a test (A1).

A1. THE VERB CATALOG IS NOT YET THE CONTRACT BETWEEN WORLDS
---------------------------------------------------------------
What exists: src/mind/data/verbs.yaml (34 verbs, `backends:` per verb). Voxel runs 14, rooms 26, both 7 (counts above). The lab inn offers 37 verbs; 13 are NOT in the catalog: accept, ask, buy, fend off, gossip, look around, offer, refuse, serve, sit down, sleep, tend, work (v: regex over lab/world2/world.py against the catalog). The decision docs count the verbs three ways: PA6 says "today's 28 verbs", the catalog has 34, the lab inn has 37. verbs.yaml is also modified and uncommitted (+81 lines: the door verbs), so there is no frozen reference.
Problem: (1) A mind chosen on the inn menu transfers to the voxel on 7 verbs. The lab's whole food, gossip and self-care work lives in verbs the voxel cannot express. (2) The learned mind was trained on a menu the catalog cannot even write (the 13 verbs), so its menus cannot be replayed through src/mind. (3) PA6's "re-express 28 verbs as shortcuts" has no agreed list to be complete against. (4) Eating exists as a body law and is not an act (policy.py:152-181 has no eat/drink/give/tell).
Better option: make the catalog the acceptance contract. (a) Add the 13 inn verbs to the catalog as data, each with a rooms program (they exist) and a voxel program or an explicit `voxel: n/a (reason)`. (b) A test prints and checks the parity table (verb x backend: program, n/a with reason, or MISSING); MISSING fails once a backend is declared to be at parity for a group. (c) The voxel programs are written ON the 16a primitives (serve = take + walk + give; sleep = lie down + wait until wake; eat = hand to mouth + the existing `eat` body law; ask/offer/accept/refuse/tell/gossip/praise/insult = speech acts through the existing sound and sense laws, with the verb's `told` text only for narration). (d) One count is used everywhere.
Cost and risk: M for the data and the test; the voxel programs ride on 16a (L, already scheduled). Risk: low for the data; the speech acts depend on the K layer for meaning (a told claim is a typed belief, K1-K3).
Scheduled or NEW: PA6 schedules "re-express the verbs"; the 13 missing verbs, the parity test and the single count are NEW.
Priority: HIGH.
Owner-level or technical: technical.

A2. BODY NEEDS ARE NOT DRIVES IN THE VOXEL; THE INN'S SELF-CARE RULE HAS NO VOXEL TWIN
---------------------------------------------------------------
What exists: the inn has seven drives (energy as hunger and thirst, safety, company, standing, wealth, rest, curiosity) with set points and NATURE weights (lab/world2/world.py `DRIVES`, `NATURE`, drift per turn `RATE`). The lab's latest fixes are one shared self-care function (`selfcare`), `serve`, `bring`, a sourced supply and the `field` energy fixes (repeat ration 60 percent, meals wasted on the full; lab/world2/world.py:329-348). The voxel has the body that makes those needs real: digestion stores, glycogen, thirst and heat balance (digestion.py, bodyheat.py:595 `DG.hunger`, bodynodes.py). But the link reports only `safety` and `harm` (mindlink.py:298) and the catalog's hunger and thirst rows say `voxel: None` (verbs.yaml `drives`).
Problem: a voxel mind never feels hungry, thirsty, cold or tired as a choosable pressure, so no self-care exists in the truth world and the inn's food results cannot be checked against it. The owner's SC1 ("the same body drives for everyone") is decided and has no code seam yet.
Better option: one function in src/mind, `needs(self_state) -> {drive: level in 0..1}`, with each backend filling a SelfState the same way: rooms from its drive fields, voxel from the body (hunger from the gut and glycogen stores, thirst from water balance, fatigue from sleep debt and exertion, cold from the heat model, pain from the injury records, fear from the existing fear read). The inn's set points become the mapping table (data). The shared self-care rule lives next to it, backend neutral, and is tested on both. Company, standing, wealth and curiosity stay mind-side (no body); they are driven by percepts (A4).
Cost and risk: M. Risk: the inn's drive drift is per turn; it must be re-expressed per second (D15 of the engine audit) before it is compared to a body that runs in joules and grams. A unit test that holds the rooms drift to the voxel body's hunger curve over a day is the check (a first agreement test, A5).
Scheduled or NEW: SC1 and SC6 are decided; 16a schedules the body clock and the self-care dial. The needs function, the shared SelfState mapping and the agreement test are NEW.
Priority: HIGH (the learned mind's survival selection is only meaningful if the voxel can ask the same question).
Owner-level or technical: technical (the principle is decided).

A4. THE SOCIAL LAWS LIVE IN THE WORLD OBJECT, ONCE, IN THE LAB; THE VOXEL HAS NONE
---------------------------------------------------------------
What exists: in the inn, liking, anger, trust, reputation, wear, reprisal and gossip are applied by the WORLD in `_perceive`, `_learn`, `_learn_flag`, `_provoked`, `_witness`, `_after` (lab/world2/world.py:1672-1930), as fields on `Person`. The learned mind keeps its own copy of the same quantities mind-side (lab/mindL/concerns.py, partners.py, drives.py, mind.py). src/core/social.py has a third (an `EDGE_KINDS = {disposition, fear, trust}` store on the graph, social.py:24). The voxel has beliefs with sources (beliefs.py) and a `told` channel, but no liking, trust, anger, reputation, debt or concern store (grep: no `liking`, `reputation`, `gossip` in src/voxel).
Problem: (1) The five quarrel causes found in the inn and the sourced concern rates (Hall 2018: about 94, 164 and 219 hours to casual, friend and close friend) have no home that both worlds can use. (2) Putting appraisal in the world breaks the owner's no-god-channel rule: a world-side `liking` is a fact no one perceived. (3) The same word is used for two things: voxel `stance` is a body posture (87 files) while social stance is an attitude (core/social.py `STANCE`).
Better option: a backend-neutral relations store in src/mind (`relations.py`): (a) state per ordered pair (liking, trust, anger, debts, ownership claims, reputation heard) held in the Mind as typed beliefs (K1, K2), updated ONLY by percepts (saw, heard, told, did); (b) the inn's laws as pure functions (percept, state) -> state, each with its constants per second or per contact hour, not per turn; (c) rooms and voxel only emit percepts and run verbs. The inn laws are then tested once against the five causes and the Hall rates; the voxel gets them for free the day it emits the percepts. Name the quantity `attitude`, so `stance` stays posture.
Cost and risk: M-L. Risk: the learned mind (lane B) already carries its own appraisal terms; the boundary must be decided with lane B so the rules-level laws live in src/mind and the learned mind READS them (a rule of the world, not a rule of the learner). Risk of recalibrating the inn: the per-turn constants must pass the same flags-on goldens after conversion.
Scheduled or NEW: partly scheduled (K1-K12 for beliefs; the family step for concerns; AG1 for claims). The home (mind-side, percept-fed), the unit rule and the rename are NEW.
Priority: HIGH.
Owner-level or technical: technical with a principle at stake (no god-channel; appraisal belongs to the mind). I would ask the owner for a one-line nod because it moves code the lab inn owns today.

A5. ROOMS AS THE VALIDATED COARSE TWIN: WHAT IT HAS TO BE, AND WHAT IT CANNOT BE
---------------------------------------------------------------
What exists: the SC5 contract (applicable, horizon, advance with all ledgers booked, materialise consistent with what was seen; exact and approximate grades; one shared harness). The voxel has the machinery (slowtwins.py, twinharness.py, slowtime.py, slownet.py). The voxel has an inn-night scene with real bodies (scenes_slow.py:184, "THE INN NIGHT WITH REAL BODIES") and the inn runs 300 turns a night. The rooms world has no energy ledger in SI units, no horizon, no materialisation, and its constants are per turn. The inn's "night" is a day: 300 turns of 288 s is 86,400 s (the lab says so at world.py:244, "One night is one day"), while the voxel inn night is hours.
Problem: (1) Without a stated unit the two worlds cannot be compared at all; D15 of the engine audit names it. (2) Some inn laws have no fine truth to be a twin of: the market, the supply of food, a bandit raid, an ailment episode, gossip at village scale. The voxel has no economy, no crops, no animals, no weather (EA5: the outdoors is later). A coarse law with no fine counterpart cannot be validated by an agreement test; it can only be a labelled stand-in. (3) The twin's HORIZON (when to hand back to the voxel) has no definition for a world of rooms: an observer entering, a fire, a blow, a rare event.
Better option: split the rooms world's laws in two classes and state it in the code.
  CLASS T (twin laws): hunger and thirst balance, sleep, warmth in a shut room, injury and bleeding rates, who is in which room, who saw what. Checked against voxel runs of the same cast and the same hours: facts (who is fed by hour N, hunger distribution, who woke, who was hurt, who knows what) within a stated budget, via the shared twin harness. The first agreement test is the inn-night scene (voxel) against the rooms night (cast of 4-5, 8 hours, no violence).
  CLASS S (stand-in laws): food supply, catch luck, raids, ailments, hardship. Each is a labelled calibration to a source (the Library responses), recorded as a row in a stand-in table with its source and the process in the voxel that will REPLACE it when that exists. They are never called "twin".
  Define the horizon list: a person enters the observed room; fire or smoke; a blow, a fall or a wound; a threshold flip (a reserve at zero); a rare-event draw; the player's attention. Materialise: when the player arrives, the coarse state becomes voxel placements consistent with what was seen (K10), using the oracle idea from core (A7).
  Put the unit rule in code: seconds are the unit; a turn is a stated jump length (D15a), and `Turn.seconds` is passed to every rate.
Cost and risk: L (the agreement test and the unit conversion M; horizon and materialise L). Risk: the voxel inn night may not match the inn's hunger and fatigue on the first try; that is the point of the test, and the finding feeds the calibrations. Risk of over-claiming: calling a stand-in law a twin.
Scheduled or NEW: SC5, SC7, Fam2 and F24 schedule the twin at the family step. The class T/S split, the stand-in table and the horizon list are NEW.
Priority: HIGH for the unit rule and the first agreement test (they invalidate selection runs if wrong); the full twin can wait for the family step.
Owner-level or technical: OWNER-LEVEL for one question: is rooms a permanent LAB INSTRUMENT (cheap, isolated, tuned for learning) or an offscreen part of the GAME (a twin the player can meet)? My recommendation: both, in this order. Instrument first (its value does not depend on twin-hood). It becomes a game part only for the T laws and only after its agreement test passes. The S laws stay an instrument until a fine world replaces them. This fits SC5's grades (exact and approximate) and keeps the lab's learning runs cheap.

A6. THE INN IS HARD-CODED; THE COARSE WORLD SHOULD BE A GENERIC ROOM-GRAPH ENGINE OVER A SCENE PACK
---------------------------------------------------------------
What exists: ROOMS, TRAITS, KINDS, CAST, THINGS, WEAPON, CHEST, DUR and the effects of every verb (`_apply`: `if v == "take"` ...) are Python in world.py (rooms/world.py and lab/world2/world.py:171-200 and 489+). voxel scenes are Python too (scenes*.py, 3,466 lines; each `scene_*` builds a World by code).
Problem: (1) Content as data is a standing rule and AU5 moves materials, verbs, species and body sheets only. Scenes and casts are not on the list. (2) A coarse twin of a voxel scene needs the room graph, the cast and the things DERIVED from that scene (rooms from walls and doors; the voxel routing code already builds routes); a hard-coded inn cannot be derived from anything. (3) A second scene (a village, a dungeon level, the family's house) needs a fork of a 2,000-line file.
Better option: a YAML scene pack for the coarse world (rooms and edges with door states, kinds with trait means, things with properties and holders, cast with sheets, supply dials), validated by the AU4 checker, plus a one-way exporter from a voxel scene (rooms from the routing graph and the labelled spaces, cast from persons, things from entities). The inn becomes the first pack. Verb EFFECTS stay code (laws are code); each reads the verb's catalog entry for roles and durations (A1). Do the same for the voxel scenes in AU5's stage (a scene file format), since the exporter needs a source of truth.
Cost and risk: M for the inn as a pack; L for the exporter and a scene format in voxel. Risk: low for the pack (the flags-off goldens prove it), medium for the exporter (it defines what "a room" is in voxel).
Scheduled or NEW: AU1/AU5 cover content as data in general; scenes and casts as data, and the rooms exporter, are NEW.
Priority: MED now, HIGH when the twin starts (A5) or a second scene is wanted.
Owner-level or technical: technical.

A13. OWNERSHIP, VALUE AND THEFT: THE INN HAS A GOD FIELD, THE VOXEL HAS NOTHING
