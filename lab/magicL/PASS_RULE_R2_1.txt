PASS RULE R2.1 (written BEFORE any measurement run; 2026-11-24)
Model: Hamiltonian compact U(1) lattice gauge theory + compact matter phase, periodic cubic lattice.
  H = sum_l E_l^2/2 + K sum_p (1-cos theta_p) + sum_i pi_i^2/(2 chi) - J sum_l cos(phi_i - phi_j - theta_ij)
  Sources: Kogut and Susskind 1975 (Hamiltonian form); Fradkin and Shenker 1979 (matter, Higgs regime);
  Creutz, Jacobs, Rebbi 1979 (4D U(1) phase transition); Polyakov 1977 (monopoles in 3D); classical real-time
  lattice gauge practice (Moore, Hu, Muller; Ambjorn et al, 1990s) for leapfrog with exact Gauss law.
  Gauss law: G_i = div E_i + pi_i - rho_ext_i, kept at its initial value to round-off (pass: max|G-G0| < 1e-9).
  Energy: leapfrog, error O(dt^2); pass if relative energy fluctuation < 1e-2 and no secular drift above 1e-4 per 1e5 steps at dt=0.02.
Units: T=1 unless stated (equilibrium depends only on K/T and J/T). chi = matter susceptibility.
Thermostat: Andersen-type refresh of (E,pi) from the Gaussian conditioned on Gauss law (exact projection); keeps Gauss law and
  gives a canonical state. Wave runs are microcanonical after thermalisation. Seeds: 11, 22, 33 (3 seeds per point unless stated).
Lattices: 8^3, 12^3, 16^3 (24^3 only for one dispersion check if affordable).
Uncertainty: blocked time series (>=10 blocks) per seed; then the spread across the 3 seeds; quoted error = larger of the two.
Verdict words: SUPPORTS, REFUTES, UNCLEAR. Nothing is tuned after seeing results; if a parameter is changed, the report says so.

(a) WAVES (pure gauge field K, T, no matter, and with matter)
  Measure: peak of the power spectrum of the transverse E mode with k=2*pi*n/L (n=0..3), omega(k).
  SUPPORTS H1 (Coulomb phase has linear waves): omega/(2 sin(k/2)) constant within 10% over n=1..3 for K/T>=2, and
    c^2 = K <cos theta_p> within 15%, so c grows as sqrt(K) at weak coupling.
  REFUTES: omega not linear in k, a gap with no matter, or c not depending on K.
  Matter with J>0: SUPPORTS the Higgs picture if a photon gap omega(0) > 0 appears and grows with J (expected omega0^2 ~ J<cos d>).
(b) FORCE LAW (two static opposite charges at separation r along an axis)
  Observable: amplitude A = <E,E_c>/<E_c,E_c> of the measured time-averaged field on the prediction E_c of the Gauss-law
  minimiser, and the mean axial field between the charges, against (i) Coulomb/Yukawa E_c (A=1), (ii) a string (mean axial field = 1,
  independent of r). Energy proxy U(r) = A^2 U_pred(r).
  SUPPORTS Coulomb: pure gauge A=1 within 0.05 at all K,T tested, profile Coulomb. SUPPORTS screening: with matter (chi>0) A=1
    with a Yukawa U_pred (screening length 1/sqrt(chi)), meaning exponential fall-off of the field.
  SUPPORTS confinement (a string tension): U(r) rising linearly, axial field independent of r and near 1 at large r, for K/T small
    where monopoles proliferate. REFUTES it if A stays 1 with the Coulomb profile at all K/T and J (an exact argument predicts this:
    in classical equilibrium E and pi are Gaussian and decouple from theta and phi).
(c) CONFINEMENT / MONOPOLES
  Observable: monopole density per cube vs K/T (3D classical, with and without matter), and in a 4D Euclidean control (the
    quantum ground-state analogue, Metropolis, Wilson loops, Creutz ratios chi(2,2), chi(3,3)).
  SUPPORTS H1's picture of a monopole-driven confining regime if monopole density rises steeply at small K/T AND the Creutz ratio
    is positive there (4D control) AND vanishes at large coupling K.
  Polyakov check: in 3D, ln(density) falls roughly linearly in K/T (no sharp transition); a jump would be a phase transition.
(d) SCREENING AND FLUX TUBES (Higgs regime, large J)
  Static: field of a test charge A=1 against a Yukawa curve, exponential with xi=1/sqrt(chi): SUPPORTS screening.
  Flux: a uniform background flux 2*pi*n_f through the xy plane, damped relaxation, 3 seeds:
    SUPPORTS quantised tubes if the number of vortices (integer winding plaquettes) equals n_f exactly and the field-energy
    participation ratio rises with J (flux gathers in tubes), the flux in the 3x3 plaquettes around each vortex rising towards 2*pi
    as J/K grows. REFUTES if vortex number != n_f, or flux stays uniform at large J.
(e) PHASE MAP over (K at T=1, J), L=12:
  order parameters: plaquette <cos theta_p>, monopole density, Higgs link <cos d>, vortex density (fraction of plaquettes with |v|>0),
  photon gap omega(0). Labels fixed now: weak/Coulomb-like if <cos theta_p> > 0.8 and <cos d> < 0.5 ;
  Higgs if <cos d> > 0.7 ; strong/disordered if <cos theta_p> < 0.5 and <cos d> < 0.5 ; otherwise crossover.
  SUPPORTS H1 if three distinct regions appear whose boundaries sharpen with size; a pure smooth crossover with no sharpening is
  reported as UNCLEAR (Fradkin-Shenker and Polyakov predict crossovers in 3D classical).
