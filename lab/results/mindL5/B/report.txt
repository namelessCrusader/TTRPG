3. WHAT THE FAILURES POINT AT
- THE MIND: all learners die of thirst by about night 5 to 15, with and without concerns. The learned mind has not learned a drink-before-the-reserve-runs-out policy in its own life of this world (naive learners; matches stage A: 6 of 7 dead within 41 nights, old inn 3 of 5 within 16). The rules have a self-care law the learners lack or learn too slowly. This is the thing AQ4 asked about, and it is the first blocker: no concern, kin rule or bond can matter if the person is dead by night 15. A warm start (pre-trained drink and eat policy) or the self-care law as a prior on the learned mind is the next step; per the owner's rule it must be a general drive mechanism, not a special case.
- THE PAST: the concerns saturate (stage A), so even when minds live the kin tilt is weak (0.59) and not due to the concerns.
- NOT THE WORLD: the rules household lives in it (0.95 percent dead), with a clean food supply (field flag).
