2. THE KIN RULE
- History H = 1 - exp(-N/(5 years)) of early-start-weighted co-residence nights; it slows fade and scales the reciprocity coupling (x6 at full history, the Ache slope).
- Earlier start counts more: each night weighted max(0.25, 1 - 0.025 x my age at the start), no hard window.
- Observed-care cue: seeing someone I care about (weight 0.30 to 0.50) care for T raises my kin cue for T.
- Kin estimate khat = 1 - (1-k_y)(1-k_c): k_y from years lived with T under 18 (95% at 15 years, the 14-18 crossing), k_c the care cue. It multiplies the friendship weight by 1 + 0.70 x capacity dial x khat. Capacity, learning-rate and fade dials are per person (12% slow-fade tail).
- Tests (test_kin, 11 pass): 15 years from birth khat 0.95; 15 years as an adult 0; start at 2 gives 0.78 against 0.70 at 10; a cared-about carer moves the cue to 0.95, an indifferent one to 0.
