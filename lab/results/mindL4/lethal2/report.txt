THE THREE FIXES (each behind its own flag; flags off = exactly as before; the five lab suites pass: test_world, test_mind with golden traces, test_tournament, test_effects, test_concerns, plus new test_survival)
1. bring. ONE act, `serve`, now brings any consumable to a person who cannot fetch it: a portion from a pot, or a drink from a source (well, barrel) or a loose drink. The cost is the walk there and back. The laid-up can ask for a drink as they ask for a meal. No per-item case.
   Result: no thirst deaths at all in 60 lifetimes (before: 34 thirst deaths, 33 of them laid-up people).
2. raidlaw. Cause found: a raid kept striking people who were already down until they were dead (about 25 blows a raid, 1.6 deaths a raid). Now blows go only to those still standing, and the raiders leave when nobody stands. With only that change: 0 deaths in 300 raids. The source's unit is the RAID (Turkana, 5 x 0.011 = 0.055 deaths a raid), so a raid is a killing raid with chance 0.055 (its first blow is mortal). This is one explicit constant read from the source, not a hidden cap, but it IS a calibration to the source unit, not an emergent result of blows. Check: forced killing raids kill 49 of 50 (so about 0.054 deaths a raid); 0 of 300 without the killing draw.
   In the 30-seed set the raid nights are the same for every chooser: 61 raids, expected 3.4 killing, observed 0 (p = 0.035, chance). So raids show no deaths in the sweep below.
3. selfcare. The lethal-only patch in partners.py is gone. One shared function (policy.self_care: ask, offer, buy, work for your own food or drink) is applied to every character; partner scripts keep only how they answer others. Flag off: partner tests unchanged.
