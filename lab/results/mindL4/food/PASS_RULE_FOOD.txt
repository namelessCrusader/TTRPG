PASS RULE AND RISK MEASURE (written 2026-11-22 BEFORE any food-world survival sweep; nothing is tuned toward it).
World all6hl2f = all6hl2 + field (food supply and bandit arrival set from the sources, see world.py FIELD block).
730 nights a life, seeds 1-30 (more if time allows, never fewer), focals Tom / Sir Ada / Marta by seed, Fenn = P-need(0.9), Oren = P-hard(0.9).
Choosers: rules (partner-blind) and oracle_both (knows both partners' rules). P-kind(0.9) Fenn is run afterwards for comparison only.

THE RISK MEASURE (per life, read from the reserve record of every night, passive)
  Every person has a hunger reserve (44.25 days) and a thirst reserve (4.44 days). Death is a reserve at zero.
  f(t) = the LOWEST of the two reserve fractions during night t (reserve / its full value; 1 = full, 0 = dead).
  DEPLETION (PRIMARY) = the mean over all 730 nights of (1 - f(t)); a night after death counts as 1 (dead = fully depleted), so an early death cannot make a life look safe.
  Secondary, no pass role:
    NEAR-MISS nights per 1,000 = nights with f(t) < 0.20 (a dead night counts).
    BURN = days of reserve drawn down per year (gross draw, all clocks).
    EXTREME-NIGHT share = nights with hunger held at its cap for at least one turn (thirst is counted separately).
    ESTIMATED DEATH RISK = for each person-night, the arm-blind empirical probability of dying within the next 30 nights given its f(t) bin
       (bins <0.05, 0.05-0.1, 0.1-0.2, 0.2-0.4, >=0.4, fitted on all five cast members of BOTH arms pooled); a life's risk = the mean over its nights x 365/30 (a rate a year).
       Reported only if the pooled sample has at least 15 deaths.
    Cox hazard ratio on focal deaths (stratified by seed; the old statistic), with its profile CI, as the secondary test on deaths.
    The whole cast's DEPLETION (all five people, both arms), and deaths by cause per 1,000 person-years against the library.

PASS: for each seed, d = DEPLETION(oracle_both) - DEPLETION(rules) (paired by seed). The mean of d is below 0 AND the 95 percent interval of the mean excludes 0
  by BOTH a paired t interval (n-1 degrees of freedom) AND a bootstrap over seeds (2,000 resamples, percentile). A Wilcoxon signed-rank p is shown as a check.
  NOT MET otherwise. If both arms have zero depletion in more than 80 percent of seeds the verdict is NO EXPOSURE (the world does not starve the focal, so the
  question cannot be asked there), and says so.
