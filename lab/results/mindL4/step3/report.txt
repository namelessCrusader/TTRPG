0. DIAGNOSIS OF THE STEP 2 FLAG (L1 shared_early of the effects arm -0.379 [-0.602, -0.156], 0.55 worse than the labels arm)
What I checked, and what I found.
(a) Sign. error_drop(kind, need, effect) is errors(need) - errors(need + effect); a falling need is a positive worth. The state tests of step 2 pass
    (food worth 0.33 hungry, 0.00 fed). The logged windows after Fenn's give and praise show needs FALLING (energy -0.016/-0.025, wealth -0.084/-0.038), so the
    measured effect has the right sign. No sign error.
(b) Credit window. Bookkeeping read line by line: need0 and tref are the needs and the turn at the start of the step the act landed in; the window closes 12
    turns after the act; the overlap shares are the Rescorla-Wagner ones. No error found.
(c) DRIFT SUBTRACTION: a defect. The drift was updated once per TICK with rate = change / dt, so the many 1-turn ticks (asks, looks) outweighed the few long
    ones (meals, sleep). Seed 1, 60 nights, 3417 ticks without an open entry, per-turn change of the needs (energy, safety, company, standing, wealth, rest,
    curiosity), mean over ticks against the true turn-weighted rate:
      per tick   +0.0021  0.0000 +0.0008 -0.0003 +0.0033 +0.0010 +0.0001
      per turn   +0.0016 +0.00002 +0.0010 -0.0002 +0.0012 -0.0003 +0.0003
    Wealth is 2.7 times too high and rest has the wrong sign. Over a 12 to 30 turn window that is 0.01 to 0.04 of need, the size of the real effects
    (0.01 to 0.08). It is the same for every partner, so it does not make one partner look worse than another, but it moves every act's worth.
    FIX (option fx_fix, default off; effects.DeltaMeanings turn_drift): the drift is a ratio of decayed sums, change over turns.
(d) OWN ACTS IN THE WINDOW. Not subtracted, not changed. A meal after a gift is part of what the gift does (the mediator), and the rest of what I do in a window
    does not depend on who spoke to me, so it largely cancels. I did not test this beyond that argument. Still open.
(e) The partner term does contribute. Effects arm without the partner term (fxnoP, modules AKVWEC): L1 shared_early -0.011 [-0.356, +0.334]. Effects minus
    that, paired by seed: -0.368 [-0.771, +0.035].

1. WHAT WAS BUILT (lab/ only, no src/, no git)
Mind option "concerns" (default False). Settings: cn (RWConcerns settings), cn_things (default True), b_concern (1.0: the same units as the world-model lookahead,
a predicted drop of error), cn_fx_prior (cultural beliefs about what an act does to another's need). Option "fx_fix" (default False) for the drift fix.
lab/mindL/concerns.py (new): the interface Concerns, the default rule RWConcerns, the estimator OtherEffects, need_of (signs to one number).
lab/mindL/mind.py: the hooks. The mind reads the needs of people in its room through w.perceived_need (the signs: hurt, fear, hunger with the target's
expressiveness and the observer's acuity) and things it holds (need 0) or that lie loose in its room (need 1; value 1 or more). The choice gets a term
"concern" = b_concern x sum over acts at a person of weight x (relief x need read - rise). The reward of a step gets weight x (need before - need after) for each
target read at both ends. The concerns learn from the homeostatic reward only, never from their own reward.
lab/mindL/effects.py: turn_drift. lab/mindL/tests_L.py: chooser "mindx" (settings in the spec), PaidError (a control world), night rows hold the weights, world all6h.
New: test_concerns.py (10 tests), step3_runs.py, step3_report.py, step3_tables.py. Every older suite passes with the option off (below).
THE RULE, in short (docs/learned-mind-target.md 9.2)
- Every mind starts with NO concerns. A target appears at first contact (a person or thing perceived). Targets are persons and things, never acts (checked in a
  lifetime: the keys are cast names or "thing:...").
- a_T: Rescorla-Wagner association between the target's presence and the step's homeostatic reward minus its running average (conditioned reinforcement; what is
  always there or there at random predicts nothing; cues share one prediction so a placebo is held at 0).
- e_T: effort justification. If my act was aimed at T, it relieved T's need (measured, or predicted by the learned effects, at least the noticeable 0.15) and the
  step cost me (error rose), e_T += cost x the rise.
- h_T: habituation. Each turn of contact multiplies it by 0.995; it recovers with time constant 300 turns; it scales what a contact teaches.
- w_T = min(ceiling 1.0, gain 20 x max(a_T, 0) + e_T). It falls by disuse (2 percent a night), disillusion (a_T goes negative when error rises after contact, and e_T
  shrinks 5 percent a step then) and habituation. Ceiling and gain are the capacities.
- OtherEffects: what my acts do to the needs of the people I act at, per act: rho (fraction of the need relieved) and rise, from the person's reading at my act
  and when the window closes (next act at that person, or 12 turns), minus the natural change of the need learned from the stretches with no act aimed at the
  person. The change goes to the latest act only (sharing it among overlapping acts made rates blow up to the clips in my first version, and made a placebo look
  as good as the real act in step 2). A cultural prior can be set with set_prior.
All the numbers (JND 0.15, lr 0.2, baseline rate 0.05, gain 20, ceiling 1, cost 1, fade 0.02, disillusion 0.05, habituation 0.005 a turn, recovery 1/300, n0 5) were
set before any run and not tuned to pass a test. NONE IS SOURCED YET. They need sources (attachment formation and decay, habituation of social reward).
THE INTERFACE (Concerns): learn(step) -> concern reward of the step; pull(T); expected(verb, T, need) (the choice term); relief(verb, need); set_prior(verb, rho,
eta, n0); observe_read(t, readings); did(target, verb, t, need); flush(); night(); table() and set_table(t) (for inspection and the swap control).
A null Concerns plugged in runs a lifetime (tested). A local rule would replace RWConcerns: a_T is a delta rule on the contact cue (a three-factor rule with the
contact as the eligibility trace and the error drop minus the prediction as the third factor); h_T is a fast-decaying gain per synapse; OtherEffects is one
delta-rule row per act. None of that is built.

5. WHAT NEEDS MORE WORK
1. THE WORLD. Weights form at noise level (0.04) in all6s and all6h. Real kin-style need-based help (the library's next lever, KIN) or a world where a person's
   standing by changes my needs for days would give the rule something to learn. Ask the owner about KIN, as the hard-inn report says.
2. NOISE FLOOR OF CONDITIONED REINFORCEMENT. A meal (0.3) goes to whoever is near; the bandit gets a weight when relief follows a raid (relief after a threat is
   conditioned to the threat: the opponent-process trap). Ideas that keep the design: score contact against what the target's own need change explains, or
   learn per place and time of day. Not done, not tuned.
3. UNSOURCED CAPACITIES: gain, ceiling, cost credit, fade, disillusion, habituation, recovery, the just-noticeable 0.15 (see section 1). A library request.
4. THINGS are targets of formation and reward only. Acts at a thing have no choice term (the menu does not name the thing). Absent targets and hearsay are not read
   (a concern for someone in another room has no pull; the hook is perceived_need's documented hearsay hook).
5. OtherEffects understates (windows close before a gift is eaten) and credits the latest act only; a decaying eligibility trace would help.
6. Own acts in the effects window (step 2 item 1) are still open (section 0d).
7. Speed: an effects+concerns lifetime of 220 nights costs about 150 to 200 s (effects alone about the same; labels 100 to 120 s in all6h).
8. fx_fix is a bug fix to a step 2 arm; the stored step 2 numbers stay as they were (option off).
