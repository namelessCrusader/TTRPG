STEP 1 OF SV5: THE WORLD CHECK FOR THE P-NEED PARTNER   2026-11-21

VERDICT
The world does NOT pay for treating Fenn and Oren differently, in the all6 inn with signs. The oracle that knows both rules does
slightly WORSE than the partner-blind rules chooser. Gap -0.011 [-0.021, -0.000], night-averaged error, seeds 1-10, paired by seed.
The pass rule (gap at least 0.1 with a CI above 0) fails. The gap is below 0.1 because the world's bound is below 0.1: 0.065
night-averaged, 0.103 generous. The world was not tuned. The old P-kind partner in all6 fails the same way (-0.004 [-0.014, +0.007],
bound 0.095 / 0.167), and its numbers equal the stored stake runs, so the new flag changed nothing for it.
So the next steps (act meanings from effects, acquired concerns) would be built on a world that, with this Fenn, does not reward
the stance they are meant to learn. See "what bounds it" and the open problems.

WHAT WAS BUILT (all in lab/, nothing in src/, no git)
1. Signs, behind the NEW world flag "signs" (lab/world2/world.py). SIGNS_FLAGS = ("signs",); world "all6s" = all6 + signs in tournament.py.
   World.perceived_need(obs, tgt) -> {hurt, fear, hunger} each 0..1, or None. reading = expressiveness(tgt) * state + noise, clipped.
   noise sd = 0.12 / acuity(obs). Expressiveness ~ N(0.7, 0.2) clipped 0.15..1.0. Acuity ~ N(1.0, 0.3) clipped 0.4..1.6. Both drawn per person
   from their OWN generator (night seed + name), carried across nights in carry_out ("dials"), so a person keeps its dials for the whole life.
   The noise is a function of (night seed, turn, observer, target): a reading repeats and the world's own generator is untouched.
   State read: hurt = max(hp loss share, ailment), fear, hunger. Only an observer in the same room who is awake and acts; the target alive.
   Hearsay (told about it) is a documented HOOK in the docstring of perceived_need, not built.
2. P-need(p), lab/world2/partners.py (class Need, make_partner "P-need:0.9"; also "need" in tests_L.make_one). Rule is in the module docstring.
   Trigger set = the P-kind triggers (give owes; ask is served; offer is accepted, each with chance p) plus the all5/all6 care acts and the words:
   serve, tend, bandage, help up, defend, praise now also owe. (fend off a raid has no target, so nothing is owed for it.)
   It pays with the menu act that best eases X's most pressing need as read through signs: reading/threshold above 1 (hunger 0.5, hurt 0.2,
   fear 0.25), largest first: hungry -> serve a portion, else give a food held; hurt -> tend, else bandage, else help up; afraid -> defend,
   else fend off the raid; nothing pressing -> praise (company). A misread pays the wrong thing. It never harms or starts. Without the flag
   it perceives nothing and pays praise. One difference from P-kind that is not about needs: P-need serves an ask for a portion (the
   `serve` verb); P-kind cannot (its ask rule only gives or tends).
   Oren stays P-hard(0.9), untouched.
3. Oracle: known["Fenn"] = "need" (tournament.Oracle). Same plan as "kind" (ask, give a small thing, walk to Fenn), except the gain
   gate also counts the focal's energy and safety error, and it may praise Fenn (a word is on P-need's trigger list and costs no thing).
   Family O takes --fenn need (spec key "fenn"; ids get a _need suffix; old ids are unchanged).
4. Recorder.by_partner (read-only): per partner and verb, the done acts at the focal, sum of b and bpot. Written into every run.
5. lab/mindL/step1_report.py prints the tables below. lab/results/mindL4/step1/diag_payments.py is the payment diagnostic.

WHAT BOUNDS IT (measured, not tuned)
1. Fenn pays almost only praise. In a diagnostic of 1,828 P-need payments (3 seeds x 60 nights, rules chooser), 98% were praise (the
   "nothing pressing" answer), 15 tend, 7 bandage, 6 fend off, 2 defend, and no food at all. Praise changes only liking, so it drops the
   focal's drive error by 0.
2. Pressing needs are rare and the focal can meet most of them itself. At the moment of payment the focal's true hunger was above 0.5 in 16%
   of payments, hurt above 0.2 in 3%, fear above 0.25 in 3%. Hunger as READ was above 0.5 in only 9%: with mean expressiveness 0.7 a hunger of
   0.6 reads 0.42. In 285 praise payments the target was truly hungry above 0.5: Fenn either misread it or held no food to serve.
3. So per answer the drop is 0.0009 to 0.0012, against 0.0016 to 0.0027 for P-kind, and the oracle's 21 answers a night (praise, mostly)
   buy nothing; the oracle's extra asks, gifts and words cost it time, which is why its gap is slightly negative.
4. The gap bound is acts x (b - c): 4.23 acts x 0.0152 = 0.065, under 0.1 whatever the chooser. The old world's bound was 0.095.

THE ORACLE DOES TREAT FENN AND OREN DIFFERENTLY: kind acts at Fenn 15.3 a night against 0.4 at Oren, a contrast 11.7 [10.9, 12.5] above
rules. It is the world, not the chooser, that fails to pay for it. For L2 and L1 (does a MIND learn to treat Fenn better) the pay-off
signal available to a learner is 0.0009 a answer: too weak for the check to say anything about the mind.

OPEN PROBLEMS
- With this P-need the world does not pass the 0a lesson. Options for the owner (none done here, because the task says not to tune the world):
  (i) a Fenn that holds and gives food more (it holds nothing to serve most of the time: the cook and the catches are the food sources);
  (ii) needs that are larger and rarer-to-self-serve (more ailments, raids), so that being cared for is worth more than 0.02 an answer;
  (iii) a company act that moves the company need (praise changes only liking; no menu act eases `lonely` directly);
  (iv) accept that this inn cannot pay, and test acquired concerns with the learned weights by the placebo, swap and clone controls of 9.2
  instead of by the oracle gap.
- The signs dials are minimal: three states, one expressiveness and one acuity, no hearsay, no magic bond, no cultural reading.
- The thresholds of P-need (0.5, 0.2, 0.25) are written values of a scripted partner; the diagnostic shows hunger is mostly misread under
  them, so the choice matters. Not tuned.
- P-need also serves asks for portions where P-kind cannot, a difference that is not the need rule. It is small (serve 0.004 a night).
- Per-answer drop for Oren is not measurable: it answers about once in 100 nights.
- All runs are 100 nights; the L tests use 300 to 400.
