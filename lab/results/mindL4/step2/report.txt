VERDICT
The mechanism works on its own controls (9 of 9 mechanism tests pass). In the all6s inn it does not yet change behaviour the right way: L1 and L2 fail for
the effects arm, as they do for the labels arm. The world pays little (bound 0.065), so this is read as information, not as a failure of step 2.
The effects arm DOES learn the right meaning: Fenn's acts are worth +0.014 to +0.019 to it (ask, give, insult), Oren's -0.004 to -0.015. These are small
numbers next to the other terms of the choice, so they do not move behaviour.

WHAT WAS BUILT (lab/ only, no src/, no git)
Mind option "effects" (default False; fx_prior, fx_log are its settings). lab/mindL/effects.py (new): the interface ActMeanings and the default rule DeltaMeanings.
lab/mindL/mind.py: with the option on, the partner term is sum over their likely answers of (chance * drop of my weighted drive error from the answer's
effect, in my current state). KIND and HARM lists are not read; the got_kind and got_harm cues are not made; b_trust must be 0 (asserted). Any act at a
person counts (no SOCIAL list). The labelled mind is untouched and is the control arm.

THE INTERFACE
predict(partner, my_act, state) -> {their act: chance within 30 turns}.  effect(partner, their_act, state) -> expected change of my drive needs, clipped so
a need stays in 0..1.  update(obs), obs is Did, Got, Tick or Flush.  set_prior(act, delta, n0, p, partner).
Default rule (DeltaMeanings): effect = delta rule on the MEASURED change of my needs over a credit window of 12 turns after the act, minus a learned drift
(what the needs do when nothing is done to me), overlapping acts share the credit as in Rescorla-Wagner, step 1/(n+1) never below 0.02. Two layers, per
(partner, act) and pooled over partners; a new pair starts at the pooled value. Answers: logistic per (partner, my act, their act), blended with the pooled
one; an answer is credited to my latest act at that partner (crediting every open window made a wave look as likely to be served as an ask: found by the
placebo test). A partner that never answers has no entries.
Local-rule plug-in points are in the module docstring: the credit entry is the eligibility trace of a three-factor rule; the answer layer would become a
delta-rule memory or Hebbian outer product; predictive coding would merge the two layers.
Cultural prior hook: set_prior hands in an initial effect (and chance) with pseudo-count n0; its weight after n observations is n0/(n0+n).

WHAT NEEDS MORE WORK
1. Own acts in the window (eating after a gift) confound the measured effect; a residual against the world model's own-act prediction would remove them.
2. A gift of food shows its effect only after eating; 12 turns catches the next meal, not tomorrow's. A longer window or a decaying trace.
3. Overlap sharing is an online approximation; the logged-life calibration CI is wide (4 seeds).
4. One drift vector for all states; effects are means (a partner who helps and harms with one act is one number).
5. The drive vector holds injury only through safety, and no concern for another person: step 3.
6. The weight b_partner of the new term was not tuned; the hard inn (step A) is needed before L1/L2 mean anything.
7. Speed: an effects lifetime costs about 230 s of 300 nights, against about 150 s for the labels arm (before cap).
