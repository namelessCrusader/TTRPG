WHAT THE FAILURES POINT AT
 The WORLD: no. The rules live (2 percent dead) in this same world.
 The MIND: yes, mainly. Learned arms die of thirst and hunger with water and food in the inn (rules reach it); the drives and childhood cut the failure by about two thirds from naive (55 to 27 percent dead) but 27 percent dead stays. Drinks per living night are higher in learned arms (8-13 against 4.8), so they drink often and still deplete: the learner's drinking is inefficient or badly timed, not absent. Culture's gain is on food, not thirst.
 The PAST: partly. Kin care appears weakly in the child arm only, and a thing that needs history is shaky. A 30-night childhood is short (u); B1 saw no difference between 30 and 90 nights in 21 lives, so more childhood is not the first thing to try.
Caveats: 15 seeds; the oldchild arm reached night 90 with a restart in the middle of the batch (no code changed); no concerns (cn2) in any arm; the half-way mark in X2 and the 30-night childhood are (u) unsourced.
