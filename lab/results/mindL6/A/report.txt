P1 SEPARATE DRIVES (letter S). lab/mindL/drives2.py. Thirst is its own drive beside hunger; energy = hunger only. The drive vector is the old seven plus thirst.
   Weight and set point of thirst = hunger's, for each kind (u: no source for a difference; the two needs differ in the body's reserve, 5 days against 45).
   Tests: test_thirst_is_its_own_drive_and_a_drink_pays_when_hunger_is_higher (old drive: the same drink earns exactly 0; new: it pays),

P2 CONVEX HOMEOSTATIC DRIVE (letter C). Keramati and Gutkin 2014, eLife, doi:10.7554/eLife.04811: D = (sum |h* - h|^n)^(1/m), n > m > 1, reward = D(t) - D(t+1).
   Exponents n = 4, m = 3 are (u): from memory of the paper's simulations, NOT confirmed against the text (the page would not load). The condition n > m > 1
   is enforced in code. The deficit of each drive is the old weighted error above its set point. The mind uses the exact drop D(now) - D(after), not the gradient.
   Alliesthesia (Cabanac 1971): the same relief is worth more at greater deficit. Tests: test_alliesthesia_relief_is_worth_more_at_greater_deficit (at least
   1.3 times, three kinds), test_the_larger_deficit_wins_when_two_compete (both ways round), test_exponents_obey_keramati_gutkin_condition.

P3 FELT DEPLETION (letter F). The body's reserve reaches the mind. dep = 1 - reserve / full reserve (0..1), read from p.reserve only. The felt need is
   need + FELT_GAIN * dep, so it goes up to 2 when the need is at its cap and the reserve drains. It is in the state needs (so in the drive and the reward)
   and in two extra state channels (dep_thirst, dep_hunger). FELT_GAIN = 1 (u) and the linear time course (u). Real anchors from memory (u): thirst at 1 to 2
   percent of body water lost, cognition drops at 2 to 5 percent, collapse near 10, death near 15 to 20 (Adolph 1947; Sawka et al. 2007). A library answer on
   dehydration symptoms by percent lost would replace the linear course.
   Tests: test_felt_depletion_grows_with_the_reserve_lost_and_reads_only_the_body, test_felt_urgency_beats_the_unfelt_drive_near_death.

P4 CULTURAL CHANNEL 1, OWN-ACT PRIORS. lab/mindL/world_model.py WorldModelP and mind option wm_prior; drives2.told is the telling. A prior is a matrix with n0
   pseudo-counts; step of a verb = lr (cnt+1)/(cnt+1+n0), so the weight of the prior after n experiences is n0/(n0+n): experience overrides it. n0 = 0 is exactly
   the old rule. Being told: lossy by design (K design). Constants all (u): T_CHANCE 0.6 (take-up chance; own experience = 1), N0_TOLD 3 (own experience counts 1
   each, no cap), T_NOISE 0.25 (relative sd of the error in what is passed on), MIN_KNOWN 3 (a teller passes on only what it has had 3 experiences of).
   Tests: test_prior_is_used_then_overridden_by_experience, test_no_prior_is_the_old_world_model_bit_for_bit, test_told_is_lossy_and_lighter_than_experience.

P5 CULTURAL CHANNEL 2, LEARNING BY WATCHING (letter W). The world flag "drives2" (with signs) adds a THIRST sign to the visible signs, as the last key so the
   other signs' noise draws are unchanged. When I perceive another person's act, and it is the only act of that person since my last reading of them, the change in
   their signs (hunger, thirst, hurt, fear) updates my own world model for that act at weight w_obs = 0.3 (u) of an own experience, on the needs I can see only
   (a mask). Optional dials copy_kin and copy_succ (default 0, u). The reading is as noisy as the world's signs. Lossy by design.
   Tests: test_watching_updates_my_model_at_a_weight_below_my_own, test_watching_in_the_world_teaches_a_mind_that_never_drank.
   Not used in the diagnostic (letter W off: the diagnostic is the naive adult).

P6 NEED BY DESTINATION (letter D). A walk or flee option carries one extra feature: the relief my OWN world model expects from the best act I remember being
   on offer in the destination room, for my CURRENT needs (a one-step lookahead over remembered affordances; every need, same law). It is also a score term
   (b_dest = 1, u: the units of the lookahead). A remembered option's "need" number is rescaled by how needy I am now against then, capped at 4 (u, a general
   stand-in for recomputing the option's numbers).
   Tests: test_walk_carries_the_relief_my_model_expects_at_the_destination_for_any_need (thirsty: the water room; tired: the other room; no need: 0; ignorant mind: 0),
   test_the_walk_option_gets_the_feature_and_the_term, test_walk_option_carries_destination_relief_in_the_menu (added this round).

P7 SAVE AND LOAD. Mind.dump / Mind.load and PerCharacter.save / load: actor, critic, world model with priors, effects, concerns, valence, habit, partner models,
