# Scene creativity (layer 3) and type behaviour, by policy

`surprising, in character` = choices an average character would make less than 1 time in 20, that this character's own traits make MORE likely. `odd` = the same, but its traits do not explain them. Story patterns: matches per night (different people each). `variety` = how different two nights are in what each type of character did (0 = the same, 1 = nothing shared). `reactions` = choices aimed at the people of an event the character just saw. Brave = share of a type's reactions to a blow or threat it saw that confront the attacker or help the victim (plan: peasant 10-25%, knight over 70%).
