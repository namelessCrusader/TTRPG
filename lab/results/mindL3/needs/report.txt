THE THREE NEEDS ONLY ANOTHER PERSON CAN MEET (2026-11-20)   user decision 1: luck first, the other three if luck is not enough

VERDICT IN ONE LINE
Three general mechanisms are built (flags tend, allies, labour), tested, and run alone and together (all5 = all4 + all three).
They change the world (illness and raids cost, variety costs) but NO pass rule flips: still 1 PASS out of 5 (always-harsh loses to
rules). The house counter still makes others matter little. Nothing was tuned: every size was fixed before the runs; the first
runs are the reported runs.

1. WHAT WAS ADDED (lab/world2/world.py, policy.py, partners.py; each its own flag; same rule for every person, no trait flag)
------------------------------------------------------------------
tend    Injuries someone else must treat. 10 percent of nights a person starts with an AILMENT (a fever, a wound on the back),
        level 0.25-0.60 on the need scale. It feeds the safety need, grows 0.0012 a turn when left alone (+0.36 a night) and is
        taken down only when ANOTHER person tends it (act "tend", 6 turns of the helper's time, helper tires 0.04, ailment -0.35).
        No one can tend itself. One may ASK another to tend it ("ask X to tend you", the existing ask path; the scripted P-kind
        partner answers it). Tending counts as a kind act in the existing tables (gossip, witnesses, trust, tit-for-tat replies).
        Draws: own generator (seed only), so every chooser gets the same ailments and the world's own draws do not move.
        Size (u, from memory): forager-horticulturalists are sick or hurt enough to stop work on about 10 percent of days (Hill and
        Hurtado 1996 Ache; Sugiyama and Chacon 2000 Shiwiar; Gurven et al. Tsimane); the badly hurt live or die by care (Sugiyama 2004).
allies  Threats that need allies. 15 percent of nights a RAID (a wolf or a raider) comes in from the yard at turn 30-200, hits one
        person in its room every 5 turns (1.8 hp x 0.5-1.5; down at hp 4, dead at -2) and walks to where the people are. Act
        "fend off" (5 turns): each finished one takes 1.5 x 0.6-1.4 off its 10 hp. One fighter needs about 6.7 rounds and is down after
        about 3.4 hits; two need 3.3 rounds and share the hits. Joining costs risk: the joiner is in the room and is hit. Score:
        bravery, allies already fighting, having been hit, fear (policy.py). Fighters who stand together like each other +0.05.
        Size (u): 15 percent a night is OUR choice, guided only by violence being about 14 percent of forager deaths (Bowles 2009, u).
labour  Division of labour (see 4 for why this form). Each person's luck catch is split by that person's own dial: the bold
        (bravery) bring GAME, the careful bring GREENS, in the share of the dial. The inn's larder is only staples. Eating has
        sensory-specific satiety: a ration of the kind just eaten relieves 60 percent of the usual (Rolls et al. 1981, variety raises
        intake by about 60 percent, u). So living on the larder alone eats at 60 percent; other kinds are in other hands.
        Needs luck. No class, no flag: the split follows the dial.
Old flags unchanged: lab.world2.test_world 56 passed (48 old, including test_flags_off_is_the_inn_as_before, + 8 new); lab.mindL.
test_tournament 15 passed. all4 re-run (rules and hermit, seeds 1-2, 100 nights) is identical to the stored luck runs (err, acts,
deaths: 3 of 3 identical). Files regress.out, test_world0.out, test_tournament.out.
Tests added (8): flags new / each alone valid / labour needs luck; ailments from own generator at the dial rate; an ailment cannot
be treated by its owner, grows, and another's tend takes it down at a cost of time and effort; an ask for care is answered by P-kind
and the score is the same for two different people; one fighter loses and two or three win, the joiner is hit, risk pools; raid
from own generator and the same facts give the same fend-off score; catch split by the dial and a repeated kind relieves 60
percent; 900 random turns in all5 stay valid.
Also in lab: tournament.py (worlds tend, allies, labour, all5, all5R0; env MINDL3_ILL, MINDL3_RAID; tend added to the always-giving
and tit-for-tat stances and to HELP_ACTS, as serve was in the inn fix), partners.py (P-kind answers an ask for care).

tend    Yes, small. Focal error on ill nights vs well nights (all4 error on the same nights in brackets), tend world:
        hermit 0.423 vs 0.369 (0.361); rules 0.555 vs 0.444 (0.440); tit-for-tat 0.611 vs 0.511 (0.420); always-giving 0.289 vs 0.270 (0.249).
        A sick night costs 0.02-0.11 more than a well one (n=82 ill nights per chooser, noisy). Who gets tended: the HERMIT is tended 0.21 times

4. WHY LABOUR TOOK THIS FORM, AND WHAT IS UNVERIFIED
------------------------------------------------------------------
First design: a sentinel (one awake in the room so that others sleep soundly; Hadza, Samson et al. 2017 (u)). Dropped before any run: in this
inn nobody sleeps (a rules person sits down for rest and the rest need is 0.004 of the error), so the mechanism would have had no hold on any
drive. A mechanism has to bind a drive that has weight. Energy (0.10-0.17) is the biggest; so labour was tied to food. The one-line lesson: a need
that ONLY another can meet matters only if it carries a large part of the error. Rest, company and standing together are 0.02 to 0.03 of it.
Unverified (u): every real-data size (10 percent ill, 14 percent violence, Rolls 60 percent, Hadza sleep); the 15 percent raid rate and the
raid strength are our choice, set from the one-fighter-loses, two-win rule only; the raid is a world event (no mind), not a person; the existing bandit
(who comes on 60 percent of nights in every world of the tournament, as bandit=None draws) is a separate threat and was left as it is.
Reprisal-off (rule 5) was run only in all5 (budget); in the single worlds only the reprisal-on extractor was run.

5. WHICH CONSEQUENCE IS STILL MISSING, AND THE HOUSE COUNTER
------------------------------------------------------------------
Did the new needs make others matter despite the house counter? Partly, and not in the way the pass rules measure.
  - Others DO matter for these needs: the counter sells nothing against a fever or a raid. Ill nights cost +0.02 to +0.11, raid nights about +0.09 and
    deaths (all5 rules 2.1 per 80 nights, before 0), and the focal that tends and fends is the one whose error falls (rules -0.022 in all5), while
    hermit gets worse (+0.032, CI above 0). A solo fighter dies; the group wins. The ally mechanism does what it was built to do.
  - But the pass rules do not move because (a) the cast makes the help nearly free for everyone: the hermit is tended as often as the helpers (0.21 vs
    0.13-0.29 a night) and is defended by the cast in a raid, so being alone is not punished; this is the same missing consequence as in the luck report,
    now seen in care and defence: kindness from the cast does not depend on what the receiver gave. (b) the partner-rule oracle's possible gain is still tiny
    (ceiling 0.017-0.029 of 0.13-0.15 error), since the oracle's scripted partners do not fight or tend by choice, and the need is unpredictable. (c) always-giving
    still beats tit-for-tat (conditional kindness has no edge when giving costs 0.02 and is answered). (d) the extractor loses anyway (harsh replies).
  - So the house counter is not the main reason any more. For the three needs the real gap is that no one is ever refused: a mechanism that withholds care from
    one who never gave it (not a rule about reciprocity, but a cost that rises with the number of times a person has been tended and has not tended, as limited time
    does for real helpers) was not built and was not asked for. Without it, a free rider survives; with it, hermit and always-giving may separate from rules.
  - Sizes matter too: if the ailment rate is 10 percent the oracle gap cannot exceed about 0.03; rule 1 (gap 0.1) needs a share of the error that only partners can
    touch of about 70 percent, and the real data do not give that. This is a threshold the rule may have to change, not a world to tune.
