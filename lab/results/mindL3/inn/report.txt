1. THE FOUR MECHANISMS (what each does; each is one rule for everybody)
------------------------------------------------------------------
meals    Food in portions. A pot of stew = 12 bowls (3.5 kg, ~290 g, ~95 kcal/100 g), a loaf = 8 slabs (100 g, 265 kcal/100 g).
         One portion = 0.25 need units = ~275 kcal (u). Supply 20 portions = 5.0 need units vs ~4.6 that five people need a
         night (all (u): the 1,100 kcal per unit is the world's own scale). Whoever holds the pot may eat a portion, SERVE one
         (a fed giver gives up one portion; a hungry receiver gains 0.25), or sell a meal for a thing worth 1-3 (buy) or for
         an hour's chores (work). Dial: World(supply=...), env MINDL3_SUPPLY.
partner  Every act aimed at a person carries the actor's trust in that person, computed from what the actor already has (its
         liking, deeds it heard, lies it caught, robberies it remembers). The score reads it the same way for everyone: kind
         acts go to the trusted, harm is held back toward the trusted. Not authored for the learned mind.
news     Gossip chosen by salience (how unusual the deed is among those the teller has seen, how much it goes against the
         teller's opinion of the doer, how big), whatever its sign. A witness warms to one who helps in view, as it already
         cooled to one who harmed. Memory keeps the rarer kind of deed.
calm     Causes found, not a number lowered (see 3).

Generality check (done by reading the diff): no mechanism names the learned mind or a character; no trait flag; all go through
the existing options/score/effect paths; flags off gives the old inn byte for byte (test_flags_off_is_the_inn_as_before passes).
Caveat to keep in mind: "partner" is a trust-weighted preference. It makes reciprocity AROUND (kind is answered 55 percent), but
the rule itself says only "prefer whom you trust".

Reading: the causes were in the world, not the score constants:
  - Charges were repeated because the habit memory of what one had said forgot after 40 turns (every believer accused again). Echo
    memory (fades with a half-life of 150 turns) is the biggest single effect (without it 13.5 harsh acts).
  - Reprisal cooled every witness to one who HIT BACK as much as to the one who began it. That is the main driver of the liking
    spiral (without the provoked-share term, liking -0.46).
  - A blow never spent the anger behind it; acting now spends half (small effect, 0.8 harsh acts).
  - Wounded and lonely terms are small. Moot charges (the thing given back) are handled by a feature.
Still 5 harsh acts a person a night remain (real remaining causes: hunger in the first hours, the unresolved first blow, accusations
that have a real thief behind them). The ablation was NOT used to tune; the numbers are the pre-chosen constants.
Caveat: removing "meals" makes the harsh count a bit worse (5.56) and liking much worse (-0.42): food scarcity is part of the cause too.
