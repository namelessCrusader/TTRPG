R2.1 REPORT: first toy experiment for H1 (classical compact U(1) lattice gauge theory + matter, with inertia)
Date 2026-11-24. Code: lab/magicL/ (lgt.py, euclid4d.py, experiments.py). Pre-registration: lab/magicL/PASS_RULE_R2_1.txt (written before any run).
Raw JSON: lab/results/magicL/R2_1/. Python: numpy+numba. No git. About 40 minutes of compute used (budget was 3 h), nice 19 under lab.lock and cap.sh, 900M cap.
Nothing was tuned to favour H1. Two changes were made after seeing data; both are stated below (marked CHANGE).

1. MODEL AND CHECKS
H = sum E^2/2 + K sum_p (1-cos theta_p) + sum pi^2/(2 chi) - J sum cos(phi_i - phi_j - theta_ij), periodic cubic lattice, T=1 unless said.
Source: Kogut and Susskind 1975 (Hamiltonian form), Fradkin and Shenker 1979 (matter), classical real-time lattice gauge practice.
Leapfrog kick-drift-kick. Gauss law: G = div E + pi - rho_ext. Static test charges are the fixed value of rho_ext.
Gauss law check: max |G - G0| = 1e-13 over 6e4 steps (8^3), and 2e-13 over 1.6e5 steps (16^3). Passes (limit 1e-9). It is exact by construction.
Energy (8^3, t=600, three time steps, charges present): relative fluctuation at dt=0.02 is 1.7e-5 (pure gauge) and 3.8e-5 (with matter).
It falls 4x for each halving of dt (O(dt^2)). Secular drift at dt=0.02: -1e-5 and +1.8e-5 per 1e5 steps. Passes (limit 1e-4).
All production runs used dt=0.02. The thermostat refreshes (E, pi) from the Gaussian conditioned on Gauss law (exact projection), so it keeps Gauss law.
Seeds 11, 22, 33. Errors are the larger of the seed spread and the blocked error. Lattices 8, 12, 16 (24 was not needed; skipped).

2. TWO EXACT RESULTS FOUND WHILE BUILDING (they change what the force-law test can mean)
(i) The Coulomb part of E cannot feel the gauge field. Pure gauge: the part of E fixed by Gauss law has zero curl, so it never enters the plaquette force.
A test charge pair therefore gives exactly the Coulomb field, whatever K, T or the monopole density. The amplitude A = <E,E_c>/<E_c,E_c> is identically 1.
CHANGE 1: A is a code check, not a physical test, so I replaced it by the local mean field (axial field between the charges, induced matter charge).
(ii) In classical equilibrium E and pi are Gaussian and decouple from theta and phi. The static potential is exactly U = (1/2) rho (-Laplacian + chi)^-1 rho.
It is Coulomb for chi=0 and Yukawa with screening length 1/sqrt(chi) for chi>0, with NO dependence on K, J, T or phase.
With the equilibrium charge insertion, paired runs (same seed, with and without charges) agree to 1e-10 in axial field and induced charge, and plaquette and Higgs link change by <1e-3 (consistent with zero).
This is an exact symmetry (a time-dependent gauge rotation), not a statistical result.

3. OBSERVABLES AND VERDICTS
(a) WAVES. SUPPORTS (with one important limit).
Pure gauge, 16^3: omega = c * 2 sin(k/2) for n=1,2,3 with c constant to 0.1%. c = sqrt(K <cos theta_p>) to within 1%:
 K=2: c=1.25 (pred 1.27). K=4: 1.908 (1.913). K=8: 2.770 (2.770). K=16: 3.960 (3.959). K=4,T=0.25: 1.979 (1.980). Errors on c are below 0.003.
So c grows as sqrt(K) at weak coupling (K/T >= 2). The pre-registered limits (linear, 10% in n, 15% in c^2) are met.
LIMIT: at K/T <= 1 (plaquette <= 0.48, monopole density 0.19 per cube) there is no propagating peak (K=1,T=1; K=4,T=4; K=0.5): the spectrum has only a low-frequency hump near omega < 0.15, where mean-field gives 0.35-0.7. The waves die in the disordered regime.
With matter: omega^2 = m^2 + c^2 k^2, with the photon gap m^2 = J <cos d> to 0.5%: J=2: gap 1.322 (pred 1.328); J=8: 2.772 (2.772). c^2 from the n=1 mode at J=2 is 1.70 vs K<cos theta_p>=1.705.
Gap grows as sqrt(J). At J=0.5 (K=2) the gap is 0.20+-0.03 vs 0.42 predicted, so the simple formula fails near the matter transition (disordered matter). Supports the Higgs-gap picture.
(b) FORCE LAW. Coulomb SUPPORTED, screening SUPPORTED, confinement REFUTED in this model.
Pure gauge, K = 0.25, 1, 4: mean axial field equals the Coulomb prediction (paired: exact). Unpaired values: K=4: 0.222+-0.005, 0.128+-0.005, 0.091+-0.005, 0.072+-0.005 at r=2,4,6,8; prediction 0.210, 0.116, 0.079, 0.060 (the small excess is the shared sampling noise; the paired result is exact). A string would give 1 at every r.
At K=0.25 the unpaired noise is large (0.2-0.5) because the field is dominated by thermal fluctuations; the paired result is still exact.
Potential (16^3 torus, exact values, r=2,4,6,8): pure: 0.2097, 0.2317, 0.2376, 0.2390. Fits: c - a/r with a=0.0806 (1/(4 pi)=0.0796), rms 0.001; linear slope 0.0047 with rms 0.005 (worse by 5x), so the string tension is 0 within 0.005.
With matter chi=0.25: 0.1941, 0.2082, 0.2104, 0.2108: Yukawa with kappa=0.5 fits with rms 0.00013; the Coulomb form rms 0.0012; linear rms 0.0036.
Screening dynamics (charges inserted bare, matter must move): induced screening charge reaches 1.00+-0.01 of the equilibrium cloud within t<1 for J=2 and J=8 at K=0.5, 2, 8 (r=4,8). At J=0 it is exactly 0 (charges frozen, no hopping). At J=0.5 it is unclear (the paired signal is noisy, 0.3-1.9 over t=1-800). chi=0.04: same result, longer screening length (0.11345 vs 0.1159 bare at r=4).
So in the classical model, screening of a static charge is Debye-like with length 1/sqrt(chi), set by matter compressibility, not by J or the phase. Confinement of electric charge needs quantised electric flux (a quantum effect); see the 4D control below.
(c) CONFINEMENT AND MONOPOLES. 3D classical: monopoles SUPPORTED as a crossover; the force part is REFUTED (see b). 4D control: SUPPORTS H1's confinement, in the quantum (Euclidean) version.
3D classical, pure gauge, 12^3, monopole density per cube vs K (T=1): 0.40 (K=0.25), 0.34 (0.5), 0.27 (0.75), 0.19 (1), 0.054 (1.5), 5.3e-3 (2), 1.9e-5 (3), 2.6e-7+-2.6e-7 (4).
ln(density) falls smoothly: slope -2.3 per unit K between 1.5 and 2, -5.7 between 2 and 3. Density and plaquette susceptibility do not depend on L (8, 12, 16 at K=0.5, 1, 1.5), so no transition sharpens (Polyakov: always a crossover in 3D).
Matter suppresses monopoles at fixed K: K=0.5: 0.34 (J=0), 0.34 (J=0.5), 0.068 (J=2); K=1: 0.19, 0.19, 0.0080.
4D Euclidean control (8^4, Metropolis, 3 seeds): Creutz ratio chi(2,2) = 1.23, 0.89, 0.69, 0.30, 0.123, 0.106, 0.086, 0.061, 0.042 at beta = 0.6, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.05, 1.1, 1.2, 1.5, 2.0. Errors below 0.01 except 1.0 (0.009).
chi(3,3) = 2.2, 1.4, 0.61, 0.16, 0.040, 0.033, 0.028, 0.020, 0.014. Monopole density per dual link: 0.31, 0.24, 0.19, 0.091, 0.023, 0.013, 0.005, 4e-4, 1e-5.
A string tension (chi > 0.1 and growing with R^0) appears for beta <= 1.0, with proliferated monopoles, and drops steeply near beta=1.0-1.05 (Creutz, Jacobs and Rebbi: 1.01). Above it chi(R,R) falls as 1/R^2 (Coulomb: 0.042 to 0.014 from R=2 to 3 is a ratio 3, consistent with 1/R^2 within 8^4 finite-size effects). Matches the known transition.
So the confining phase of H1 exists in the 4D quantum-type version, not in the classical Hamiltonian version (b).
(d) SCREENING AND FLUX TUBES. Static screening SUPPORTED (b). Quantised tubes SUPPORTED at low J; at J >= K the tubes become thinner than the lattice and are invisible.
Flux 2 pi n_f, n_f=4, K=1, 16^3, damped relaxation after anneal, 3 seeds (all 3 agree unless stated):
 J=0.1: 4 vortices, 0 antivortices, flux in 3x3 around vortex 0.23+-0.01 of 2 pi, participation ratio 1.13.
 J=0.25: 4 vortices exactly, 0.37+-0.01, ratio 1.77.
 J=0.5: visible flux quanta 2, 0, 1 (seeds): vortex count always equals the visible flux; 0.48 of 2 pi in the 3x3 block.
 J=1, J=4: visible flux 0 in every seed (plaquette 1.000, higgs 1.000).
Vortex number equals the visible flux quantum count in every run. SUPPORTS quantisation and tube formation (flux gathers as J grows).
NOTE (not a bug, but a lattice and compactness effect): once the penetration depth sqrt(K/J) is below one lattice spacing, a 2 pi flux in one plaquette costs no energy (cos is periodic) and is invisible to B and to vortex counting. "Flux expelled" at J >= 1 means the flux went to sub-lattice tubes. The model cannot tell expulsion from sub-lattice tubes. J=0 vortex counts (about 0.3 per plaquette) are random phases and mean nothing.
CHANGE 2 (made after seeing the first flux and map results): J=0.1 and 0.5 were added to the flux test, and the sharpening test was run on the K=4 line (J 0.3-0.7) instead of K=1.
(e) PHASE MAP (12^3, 2 seeds, T=1, chi=0.25; JSON map.json has every point). Order parameters: plaquette, monopole density, Higgs link, vortex density, photon gap omega(0).
Regions found (using the pre-registered labels):
 Weak, Coulomb-like (plaquette > 0.8, Higgs link < 0.5): K >= 2 with J <= 0.5. Examples K=4,J=0: plaquette 0.913, higgs 0, gap 0, c=1.9.
 Higgs (Higgs link > 0.7): J >= 1 at K >= 1, and J >= 2 at K=0.25 to 0.5. Gap = sqrt(J <cos d>): 1.3 (J=2), 1.9 (J=4), 2.8 (J=8).
 Strong, disordered (plaquette < 0.5, Higgs link < 0.5): K <= 1 with J <= 0.5. Examples K=0.5,J=0: plaquette 0.24, monopoles 0.34, no waves.
 Crossover: elsewhere (for example K=0.25,J=2: plaquette 0.40, higgs 0.75, monopole 0.15).
Sharpening test along K=4 (J=0.3 to 0.7, L=8,12,16): the Higgs-link susceptibility peaks near J=0.5 at 3.2, 3.1, 2.5 (L=8,12,16): no growth with L. Plaquette susceptibility is flat.
Verdict: three distinct regions are seen, but the boundaries are crossovers that do not sharpen with size up to 16^3. UNCLEAR as phases (as pre-registered). The K=infinity limit is the 3D XY transition at J about 0.45, which is where the peak sits; the peak is energy-like and not expected to diverge.

4. WHAT SURPRISED ME
- The force-law test I pre-registered cannot discriminate in classical Hamiltonian dynamics: Coulomb or Yukawa, exactly, in every phase (two exact results above).
- The photon gap is exactly J<cos d> and the speed is exactly sqrt(K<cos theta_p>): the dispersion is a clean order-parameter readout.
- Waves vanish at K/T <= 1 while monopoles are at 19%: the disordered region is "no light" even though the Coulomb field of a charge is still exact.
- The compact lattice hides 2 pi flux tubes, so "dead zone expels field" and "flux enters as thin tubes" cannot be told apart below one lattice spacing.
- Matter suppresses monopoles (a Higgs region is monopole-poor).

5. H1 SCORECARD
Holds: Coulomb-phase waves with linear dispersion and c^2 = K<cos>; photon mass in the Higgs regime (m^2 = J<cos d>); screened static field (Yukawa); quantised vortex tubes with integer count; monopole proliferation at strong coupling; three regimes by order parameters; the 4D Euclidean version has the confinement transition at beta about 1.0.
Fails or is changed: confinement of static electric charge does not exist in the classical Hamiltonian-with-inertia model (exact, not statistical); the screening length is set by chi, not by J; the regime boundaries are crossovers in 3D classical; flux expulsion cannot be separated from sub-lattice tubes on the lattice.
H1 missed: (1) classical electric flux is continuous, so confinement needs quantised E (quantum or a discrete-flux classical variant); (2) the disordered regime is a different kind of region (no waves, no confinement of static charge) and may be the real "dead zone"; (3) matter mobility J is a separate dial from matter number chi: J=0 means frozen charges and no screening ever.

A classical model with integer electric flux (a Z-valued or finite-spin quantum-link / Villain Hamiltonian, with Gauss law kept) at finite temperature, plus the 3+1D quantum rotor ground state by small exact or QMC methods, to test whether confinement and the string tension return, and how the string tension depends on monopole density. Also add a second matter species so the dead-zone phase can be tested without the sub-lattice flux issue, and a 24^3 or 32^3 scan of the Higgs line for a true transition.
SKIPPED: 24^3 lattices (not needed, results are size-independent); J=0.5 screening dynamics was not resolved (needs longer runs or more seeds); no thermostat-temperature scan beyond K=4 at T=0.25, 1, 4 (equilibrium depends only on K/T and J/T, so the K scan covers it).

6. NUMBERS FOR MARK1'S EFFECTIVE LAYER (lattice units a, lattice time a/c0; T=1)
1. Wave speed per lattice unit: c = sqrt(K <cos theta_p>): 1.27 (K=2), 1.91 (K=4), 2.77 (K=8), 3.96 (K=16), uncertainty <1%. Scaling: in the world, c_world = c * a_medium / tau; set it by choosing the medium spacing a so that c_world is light-like (a few times 1e8 m/s) for casting frequencies.
2. Photon gap in screened regions: m^2 = J <cos d>: 1.32 (J=2), 1.9 (J=4), 2.77 (J=8), error <0.5%. Mass scales as 1/a; a region with J large has a cutoff frequency m c_world / a, so low-frequency magic does not enter dead zones.
3. Electric screening length: 1/sqrt(chi) lattice units (2.0 for chi=0.25, 5 for chi=0.04), exact, J-independent. In the world: xi = a/sqrt(chi).
4. String tension: 0 within 0.005 (classical). In the 4D control, Creutz chi(2,2)=0.30 at beta=1.0 and 0.89 at beta=0.8 (lattice units). Does not map until R2.2 gives a quantised-flux value.
5. Monopole density per cube: 0.19 (K=1), 0.054 (1.5), 5e-3 (2), 2e-5 (3), 3e-7 (4); falling about e^(-5.7 K) at K=2-3. Matter at J=2 lowers it 10-80x at K=0.5-2. It sets the width of the disordered regime: it becomes unimportant (below 1e-3) at K/T about 2.7.
Scaling to the world: the medium parameters K/T and J/T are dimensionless, the lengths scale with the medium spacing a (unknown, to be fixed by the target c and the screening length), and the effective-layer cell (5 cm) averages over about (5 cm / a)^3 sites, so the effective layer sees only c(K,T), m(J), xi, and the regime map, as local material properties.
